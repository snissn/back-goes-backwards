## Audit conclusion

Chapter 30 can close Book I without either suppressing the authorial program or asking that program to support claims it has not yet earned. The clean structure is a one-way dependency:

1. **Durable Book I foundation.** Living bodies receive and distribute material demand through configuration-, task-, state-, and time-dependent relations. Mechanical roles are relational. Local exposure, tissue response, finding, symptom, diagnosis, function, and outcome are not interchangeable. A mechanical history can improve inquiry while biological, psychological, behavioral, environmental, and social relations remain active.
2. **Posterior organizing lens.** Posterior structures form a large, layered, mechanically consequential anatomical and functional system. Their bounded participation in stance, gait, propulsion, lifting, balance, deceleration, transfer, and reaching justifies sustained attention to anterior–posterior distribution. It does not establish a universal posterior priority.
3. **Wider series hypothesis.** Human anatomy may possess a preferred body-wide organization associated with a still-to-be-defined whole-system efficiency relation. The current candidate is biased toward downward axial resolution, external or opening rotational organization, outward expansion, and posterior participation expected to be necessary but insufficient.

The third proposition can depend on the first two. The first two cannot depend on the third. If preferred organization is not found, the proposed modes fail to discriminate, posterior participation proves unnecessary, or the later traditional and breath correspondences fail, the central Book I framework remains intact. This packet finds no technical need for Chapter 30 to introduce a new disease, treatment, prevention, diagnostic, or practice claim.

## Proposition I — the durable Book I foundation

### What can be stated affirmatively

A living body is a material system with changing geometry, contacts, activation, pressure, capacity, and boundary conditions. Force, moment, pressure, stress, strain, work, power, stiffness, compliance, dose, capacity, and recovery name different quantities or relations. They do not collapse into a generic substance called load. A path is a bounded account of how a declared demand is shared among candidate carriers; it need not be one cable, a serial handoff, or a directly visible line.

Continuum mechanics supplies the discipline of declaring bodies, boundaries, configurations, deformation, forces, stresses, constitutive behavior, and balances. It does not by itself solve a living body's internal force-sharing problem or identify a clinically important path.[^gurtin2010] Measurements of tissue stress in living systems likewise depend on a relation among force, geometry, material response, sensors, images, and models; observing shape change is not automatically a direct stress measurement.[^gomez2020]

Book I's hard/soft distinction is an explicit project vocabulary for a division of mechanical labor. A hard role is comparatively shape-preserving, force-transmitting, constraining, or structurally resolving in the declared direction, scale, phase, and task. A soft role is comparatively form-receiving and shape-conforming: it remains able to yield, glide, contain, expand, buffer, respond to pressure, or change local shape. The terms are **motivated by TCM** hard/soft and Yin/Yang concepts but are not claimed as exact translations. They do not classify bone as always hard or anatomically soft tissue as always soft. Tendon, active muscle, ligament, fascia, cartilage, a pressurized cavity, and a coordinated assembly can exchange or combine roles.

That polarity is a synthesis model. Its value depends on whether it helps specify a system, a preserved relation, a necessary receiver freedom, candidate carriers, competing routes, and observables more clearly than ordinary stiffness, load-sharing, contact, activation, pressure, or deformation language alone. It is not validated merely because the component mechanics are real.

The foundation also includes the separation of mechanics from medical consequence. A proposed chain runs from task and environment through external demand, internal distribution, local exposure, receiver state, biological response, finding or symptom, function, clinical interpretation, and outcome. Each arrow has its own evidence burden. Causal diagrams can make assumptions, alternatives, confounding, and mediation visible, but a diagram does not establish its arrows.[^greenland1999] The World Health Organization's ICF framework independently requires attention to body functions and structures, activity, participation, health conditions, personal factors, and environmental context; it is not a constitutive model or a causal ranking for an individual.[^whoicf]

## Proposition II — the posterior organizing lens

### The strongest defensible statement

The posterior chain is real as a practical regional and functional grouping, but its borders depend on the question. It includes major structures at the foot and ankle, posterior leg and thigh, pelvis and hip, paraspinal region, thoracolumbar and shoulder-girdle relations, and posterior neck. These regions contain large muscles and mechanically consequential tendons, fasciae, joints, skeletal contacts, and active control relations. They are not one uninterrupted cable, and anatomy alone cannot establish the magnitude, direction, or clinical significance of body-wide transmission.

Whole-body MRI data support comparative discussion of muscle volume while also showing why casual rankings need care: volume is not physiological cross-sectional area, force, moment, activation, or task contribution, and large nonposterior muscles remain important comparators.[^riem2026] Morphological reviews support some proposed myofascial continuities, but functional force-transfer evidence remains limited, heterogeneous, often adjacent or indirect, and insufficient for one dominant whole-body chain.[^krause2016]

Task evidence gives the title its strongest ordinary meaning. Musculoskeletal simulation indicates phase- and speed-dependent contributions from gluteal, hamstring, plantar-flexor, vasti, and alignment-related terms during walking; it does not uniquely solve every internal force or privilege posterior structures across the gait cycle.[^liu2008] Dynamic lifting models show that estimated trunk loading depends on load, motion, asymmetry, muscle activity, and model assumptions rather than on a single visible posture.[^granata1995] Perturbed walking elicits several direction-dependent balance-recovery strategies in healthy adults, which supports posterior participation as one part of a broader response repertoire rather than a universal correction.[^karabin2024]

The Chapter 30 formulation is therefore:

> Posterior structures form a substantial, layered support and transfer system whose contribution matters in many ordinary tasks. Anterior–posterior distribution is consequently worth examining, but posterior anatomy alone does not identify a preferred route, one correct posture, a health state, or a diagnosis.

### Why posterior cannot be sufficient

A system can show strong posterior muscle activity while using a high-cost, unstable, poorly timed, or otherwise task-inappropriate strategy. It can also accomplish an important phase through anterior, lateral, deep, pressure-mediated, or external support. An external carrier can redistribute modeled loading and motion without demonstrating that the resulting pattern is globally preferred or clinically superior.[^brouwer2024] Strong posterior participation therefore cannot distinguish the proposed preferred state from every alternative.

The author's current Book II expectation is stronger: posterior participation will prove **necessary but insufficient** within the eventual domain of preferred organization. Chapter 30 may state that expectation directly because it defines the research program. It may not present the expectation as a consequence of the task evidence above. Book II must define participation, scale, phase, task domain, comparator, necessity, and counterexample before the statement can be tested.

## Proposition III — the preferred-configuration hypothesis

### Canonical provisional statement

Human anatomy may possess a preferred body-wide organization associated with a whole-system mechanical efficiency relation. In that organization, gross structural demand would resolve coherently through capable skeletal, tensile, active, and pressure-mediated support while form-receiving structures remain sufficiently free to yield, conform, glide, contain, expand, and recover. The current directional candidate combines downward axial resolution, external or opening rotational organization, outward expansion, and necessary but insufficient posterior participation.

Every important word remains an obligation:

- **Human anatomy** requires a declared population, developmental state, ability, variation, and task domain.
- **Preferred** requires a comparison class and a criterion, not aesthetic approval or one posture.
- **Body-wide** requires local-to-global transformations and cannot be inferred from one region.
- **Organization** must distinguish configuration, motion, load, activation, pressure, phase, bias, attractor behavior, and recovery.
- **Efficiency** requires an objective, denominator, constraints, and tradeoffs.
- **Coherent** requires observable continuity or coordination rather than a compelling drawing.
- **Directional signs** must specify what is signed and in which local frame.
- **Necessary but insufficient** requires both a derivation and disfavoring counterexamples.

### Preferred is not one pose

The working hypothesis concerns organization across movement, perturbation, task change, and return, not a frozen photograph. A preferred state might ultimately be represented as a family, a bias, an attractor, a set of feasible relations, or a phase-dependent routing regime. Several solutions can satisfy the same external task because internal actuation is redundant; modeled feasible activation sets provide a bounded demonstration of that nonuniqueness.[^sohn2013] Multiple viable solutions, task-specific optima, disability-specific strategies, development, training, fatigue, pain, adaptation, and environmental support therefore remain genuine alternatives rather than noise to be explained away.

### Efficiency is not yet one scalar

Candidate manifestations include strain distribution, active cost, stability, resilience, reserve, adaptability, repeatability, and return tendency. They may trade off. A strategy can minimize metabolic cost while sacrificing speed or reserve; another can increase cocontraction and robustness while increasing active demand. Even task-specific studies of tendon behavior and apparent efficiency require declared work terms and conditions and do not establish a global healthy-state metric.[^monte2021]

Book II must decide whether efficiency is:

1. one formal objective;
2. a constrained multi-objective relation;
3. a Pareto family;
4. a viability or reserve condition rather than an optimum;
5. a return or attractor property; or
6. a useful informal umbrella that should eventually be replaced.

No choice is made by Chapter 30. The hypothesis is strong enough to investigate precisely because these alternatives are visible.

### The three signed generalized modes

The proposed reduced description contains:

- an **axial mode**, currently positive toward downward resolution;
- an **opening or rotational mode**, currently positive toward external or opening organization; and
- an **expansive or lateral mode**, currently positive toward outward expansion.

This is not a basis of three Cartesian force vectors. The candidate set contains two translation-like or shape-like relations and one rotation-like relation. A generalized coordinate can describe a reduced degree of freedom; its conjugate generalized load follows through virtual work and may be a force, moment, pressure-related resultant, or another integrated quantity depending on the coordinate and system.[^mitvirtualwork] Configuration, velocity, acceleration, applied load, internal load share, and return tendency can carry different signs. Local outward directions on left and right sides can oppose one another in a global frame while sharing one bilateral expansive sign.

Book II owns:

- the exact generalized coordinates and admissible states;
- reference configurations and local frames;
- left/right and local/global transformations;
- conjugate forces, moments, or expansive resultants;
- coupling among modes;
- contact, pressure, activation, and respiratory-phase dependence;
- the distinction among instantaneous sign, phase, bias, and attractor;
- uncertainty and measurement;
- alternative reductions; and
- discriminating predictions.

Orthotropy remains an optional local constitutive approximation for a tissue or region with direction-dependent material behavior. It does not prove that the whole body is orthotropic, derive three generalized modes, or establish their preferred signs.

## Breath, pressure, geometry, and practice boundary

Ordinary pressure-mediated support is already part of Book I. Models can show that intra-abdominal pressure, posture, muscle activity, and assumptions change estimated spinal loading and stability in a bounded lifting task; they do not establish a breath-driven whole-body configuration or a breathing prescription.[^arjmand2006]

Book IV owns the stronger alternatives:

- breath as necessary driver;
- breath as major organizer;
- breath as modulator;
- breath as a measurement window or companion;
- hyoid-centered coordination;

- tensegrity, tall-torus, shell/core, rod, network, field, and other geometries;
- routing-regime transition; and
- possible formal topology only if an invariant and discontinuity justify it.

The hyoid is an anatomical node or suspended structure, not a literal fourth spatial dimension. A torus is an additional candidate representation, not a replacement imposed on the archive. “State transition” is provisional language for a materially different routing regime; it is not a claim that ordinary alignment change alters mathematical topology.

Book V owns candidate observations, tests, cues, drills, strength and coordination work, breathing practices, bandhas, yoga, Tai Chi, teaching, professional applications, and individual practice. Embodied experience can generate phenomena and hypotheses. It cannot bypass construct validity, reliability, causal evidence, adverse-event monitoring, contraindications, professional scope, or accessibility. Chapter 30 supplies no self-test or corrective routine.

- several equally viable or efficient organizations;
- no stable whole-system efficiency relation across declared tasks;
- no useful invariant or phase-consistent Yang bias;
- posterior participation is common but not necessary;

- Shape-preserving and form-receiving roles remain a testable relational vocabulary.
- Posterior anatomy remains large, layered, and functionally consequential.
