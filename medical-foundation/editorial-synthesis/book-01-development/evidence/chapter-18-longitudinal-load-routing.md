## Editorial decision

Chapter 18 can state the project's proposed addition positively without pretending that its components are absent from existing fields:

> Longitudinal load routing is a structured record of how a defined task and environment, available mechanical paths, demand distribution, local exposure, compensation, and changing state relate across time. Its proposed value is not a recovered hidden cause. It is the ability to place measurements and uncertainties held at different scales and by different disciplines into one inspectable history, then ask which observations could distinguish that history from alternatives. Many components already exist in biomechanics, ergonomics, occupational exposure science, rehabilitation, and medicine. The integrated record earns relevance only where it is reliable and adds explanation, prediction, communication, research leverage, or decision value beyond those comparators.

This is a stronger proposal than “ask about posture” and a narrower one than “medicine lacks mechanics.” Occupational biomechanics has modeled peak and cumulative loading for decades. Ergonomics already studies humans in relation to tasks, tools, organizations, and environments. Rehabilitation already distinguishes targets, treatment ingredients, mechanisms, and downstream aims. The International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health already places body state, activity, participation, and environment inside one contextual framework.[^who-icf][^iea-hfe][^hart2019]

The candidate increment is a particular *mechanical history object*: one record that follows a declared demand through changing configurations, carriers, compensations, scales, and local receivers while preserving what was directly observed, instrument-measured, calculated, modeled, inferred, hypothesized, retrospectively reported, or left unknown. Whether that object is new enough or useful enough remains an empirical question. It may be valuable in one research setting and redundant, unrecoverable, or distracting in another.

## The proposed record enters an occupied landscape

The project's novelty question should begin with overlap, not with a blank map.

The World Health Organization's ICF supplies a standard language for health and functioning at individual and population levels and explicitly includes environmental factors.[^who-icf] It is not a load-routing model, but it already prevents the project from claiming that activity, participation, environment, and bodily state have never been assembled in one medical framework.

The International Ergonomics Association defines human factors and ergonomics through interactions among people and other elements of a system. Its official account treats tasks, jobs, products, environments, organizations, tools, and technology through a holistic systems approach that includes physical, cognitive, sociotechnical, and environmental factors.[^iea-hfe] A person–environment boundary and an “upstream” interest in work design therefore belong to established ergonomics, not to this project alone.

Rehabilitation has also developed integrative specification systems. The Rehabilitation Treatment Specification System characterizes treatment components through a target, ingredients, and a hypothesized mechanism of action, and distinguishes direct targets from downstream aims.[^hart2019] Chapter 18 is not an intervention-specification system, but it shares the discipline of separating a local object, an intermediate mechanism, and a later outcome.

These comparisons narrow the claim responsibly:

| Existing object | What it already contributes | Candidate routing increment | What would erase the increment |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICF | shared description of body function, activity, participation, and environment | time-resolved mechanical paths and exposure provenance within that context | existing ICF-linked records already perform the proposed job |
| systems ergonomics | person–task–tool–organization–environment interactions and design | explicit cross-region mechanical distribution and compensation history | mature ergonomic assessment captures the same variables and decisions |
| occupational exposure science | duration, repetition, force, posture, vibration, cumulative dose, and job history | changes in internal distribution, carrier, receiver, and regional substitution | routing variables add no reliable information beyond exposure metrics |
| biomechanics | kinematics, kinetics, models, contact, work, force, and local estimates | linking selected analyses across tasks, recovery intervals, and state transitions | the proposed record merely concatenates ordinary analyses |
| rehabilitation | function, capacity, movement, adaptation, treatment mechanisms, and repeated response | pre-episode or cross-context mechanical history with explicit provenance | rehabilitation records already provide the relevant longitudinal account |
| medical specialties | tissue state, diagnosis, exclusions, local endpoints, procedures, and prognosis | connection of those endpoints to testable exposure-history hypotheses | the history does not change an explanatory, research, or clinical question |

The project can still be ambitious. It proposes that a cross-boundary mechanical history may sometimes be worth treating as a distinct research object. It cannot use the breadth of that object as evidence of novelty.

## One present endpoint can admit several histories

Use a neutral hypothetical endpoint: at the present visit or research session, a local interface is measurably changed and one task is limited. Nothing about that endpoint alone establishes which sequence preceded it.

Three histories can remain compatible:

1. **Documented local event.** A dated impact or acute failure changed the interface; later movement and support changes followed the event.
2. **Repeated exposure with compensation.** A task changed, demand recurred, the person adopted a successful alternative route or support, and the current interface accumulated a different exposure. Some intervals were observed; others are retrospectively reported or modeled.
3. **Biology or context first.** A tissue, inflammatory, neurologic, developmental, medication, sleep, stress, or participation change altered capacity and behavior; the changed mechanics followed or maintained part of the present state.

There are more than three possibilities, including mixed and unknown histories. They are not equally probable merely because they can be drawn. Their purpose is to expose an inverse problem: the current endpoint constrains possible histories but does not normally identify a unique sequence.

A routing record should therefore be ordered forward in time even when it is assembled retrospectively:

```text
time 0: person, environment, task, capacity, and available paths
    -> time 1: dated demand, event, or change in context
    -> time 2: measured or proposed distribution and local exposure
    -> time 3: response, recovery, compensation, assistance, or no change
    -> time 4: next task state and present endpoint

base evidence state at every time:
    OBS / MEAS / CALC / MOD / INF / HYP / EMB / unknown

temporal qualifier where applicable:
    prospective / contemporaneous / retrospectively reconstructed
```

A retrospective report can document what a person reported or experienced without becoming a direct observation of the historical exposure. *Reconstructed* remains a temporal qualifier: every reconstructed entry retains its underlying evidence-state label and its missing interval.

Each arrow must state a relation. A measured posture cannot silently become an internal force; a modeled force cannot silently become tissue strain; a present finding cannot prove the exposure that preceded it; and symptom relief after support cannot prove the initiating route. Hernán and Robins' causal framework is not a routing model, but its emphasis on a well-defined contrast and explicit assumptions such as exchangeability, positivity, and consistency shows why a coherent retrospective sequence is not itself an identified causal effect.[^hernan-robins]

The record gains strength when a dated measurement precedes a transition, when the proposed receiver changes with the exposure, when recovery or assistance produces the predicted difference, and when a competing history predicts something else. It weakens when missing intervals dominate, when several routes predict the same endpoint, when the proposed exposure cannot be measured reliably, or when a simpler account performs equally well.

## Cumulative exposure is established—and measurement-dependent

The archive's interest in cumulative demand is not an invention. The evidence also shows why “cumulative load” cannot remain one undifferentiated quantity.

Norman and colleagues compared peak and cumulative exposure variables in 104 automotive workers reporting low-back pain and 130 controls. The variables included model-derived lumbar moments, compression, and shear alongside measured or estimated trunk kinematics and hand forces. Four retained factors included integrated lumbar moment over a shift, usual hand force, peak modeled shear, and peak trunk velocity.[^norman1998] This supports the existence of mixed peak-and-history exposure models. It does not establish a universal cumulative metric, a unique internal route, or causation from a case–control comparison.

The EPILIFT case–control study reconstructed lifetime occupational histories for 564 people with symptomatic lumbar disc herniation, 351 with symptomatic disc narrowing, and 901 population controls. Interviews identified job histories; expert task surveys and biomechanical calculations estimated lumbosacral compressive force for handling and trunk-inclination situations. Positive dose–response relations were reported for selected cumulative occupational-load measures.[^seidler2009] The study demonstrates an ambitious longitudinal reconstruction already present in occupational medicine. Its lifetime exposure is not direct continuous observation, its compression model is not a tissue stress field, and association does not recover one individual's causal history.

Bovenzi followed 537 professional drivers for two years, combining measured vehicle vibration magnitudes, exposure duration, several cumulative vibration formulas, structured interviews, and a transition model intended to retain temporal sequence. Different exposure formulations performed differently: vibration dose value and selected lifetime cumulative measures predicted some low-back-pain outcomes better than an eight-hour root-mean-square measure.[^bovenzi2009] The result is affirmative and limiting. History and metric choice mattered, while pain, disability, measured vibration, recalled duration, physical work, and other context remained different objects.

Together, these studies establish three facts useful to Chapter 18:

- exposure science already follows physical demand through time;
- the chosen quantity and accumulation rule change the account; and
- even substantial reconstructions leave internal routing, tissue response, individual causation, and clinical utility partly or wholly unresolved.

The proposed routing record must therefore compare itself with these methods. It may add changing contacts, carriers, substitutions, regional work, or a named local receiver. If those fields cannot be measured or do not improve a declared outcome, “body-wide” is only extra narrative.

## Reconstruction is evidence with a provenance label

Retrospective history can be useful. Its limits should be recorded at the field level rather than converted into a generic disclaimer.

Spielholz and colleagues compared self-report, video observation, electrogoniometry, and electromyography-derived hand-force estimates for eighteen people performing three tree-seedling jobs. Self-reports were least precise and consistently overestimated the selected exposures; the best-performing method differed by posture or motion variable.[^spielholz2001] No single method simply became “ground truth” for every quantity.

In a cohort of automobile-manufacturing workers interviewed at baseline, one year, and six years, d'Errico and colleagues found fair-to-good one-year reproducibility for a composite exposure index and some items, but poor five- to six-year reproducibility for the composite and almost every individual exposure. Changes in work organization and regression to the mean complicated the history.[^derrico2007] The analytical samples also narrowed substantially when the comparison required the same job across longer intervals. This does not make recall worthless. It makes retrospective report a distinct source whose resolution decays unevenly across variables and time.

Job-exposure matrices trade individual detail for scalable group estimates. At the job-title level, Korshøj and colleagues compared five occupational physicians' ratings across sixteen job titles with technical or observed measures. Correlations between job-title rankings ranged from 0.55 for standing or walking to 0.83 for kneeling or squatting; estimated lifted mass was systematically higher than observation by 909 kilograms per workday, with wide limits of agreement.[^korshoj2022] A job title can therefore supply a useful exposure prior without becoming an individual's measured history.

A reconstruction table should retain at least:

| Field | Permitted source | Required disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| task and environment | record, observation, report, job matrix, device log | dates, setting, changes, aggregation level |
| configuration and contact | image, video, examination, report, model | task phase, frame, resolution, missing periods |
| external demand | instrument, object record, estimate, report | quantity, units, frequency, duration, uncertainty |
| internal distribution | calculation or specified model | boundary, inputs, assumptions, validation domain |
| local exposure | direct sensor where possible or tissue/interface model | receiver, quantity, material assumptions, dose |
| compensation or assistance | observation, report, device record, repeated test | protected function, chronology, persistence, alternatives |
| response and endpoint | measurement, report, imaging, examination | timing, meaning, discordant outcomes, competing causes |

The correct output can be a partial history. Unknown cells are information about the research problem, not an invitation to fill the route by anatomical plausibility.

## Prospective measurement can strengthen selected links

The most promising future use of the framework may be prospective rather than retrospective. Field methods can now record more task history, but accessibility and validity remain separate questions.

Greene and colleagues demonstrated a two-dimensional computer-vision method that spatially displayed the frequency, speed, and duty-cycle components of repetitive hand tasks. The method used an analyst-selected hand region and manually labeled exertion states in simulated packing tasks and industrial examples.[^greene2017] It shows one way an exposure record can preserve where and when a task component occurred. It was a method demonstration, not a validation of a whole-body internal route or individual injury risk.

Uhlrich and colleagues tested selected OpenCap outputs in ten healthy adults using two iPhones, multi-view videos synchronized during processing, pose estimation, inverse kinematics, and physics-based simulation. A separate one-hundred-participant clinician-led field experiment demonstrated a community workflow; the authors reported substantially lower hardware cost and hands-on time than their laboratory comparison.[^uhlrich2023] The outputs remain a chain from video to keypoints to a musculoskeletal model, not direct observation of muscle force or tissue stress, and the larger field experiment was not the laboratory-validation cohort.

A separate 2026 simultaneous comparison by Liang and colleagues tested forty-one participants across eight movements. Agreement with optical motion capture was stronger in the sagittal plane, while out-of-plane normalized errors and correlations were substantially more variable; the system also showed systematic angle differences and task-dependent performance.[^liang2026] This is precisely the kind of limit a routing record must retain. A scalable tool can improve prospective sampling while remaining unsuitable for a particular plane, quantity, or absolute claim.

Prospective routing research should not attempt to measure everything continuously. It should predeclare:

1. the task and outcome that make history relevant;
2. the few route or exposure variables expected to distinguish hypotheses;
3. measurement error and model sensitivity;
4. the timepoints needed to capture compensation, recovery, or state transition;
5. a comparator record without the new routing variables; and
6. the result that would show no improvement.

## The six families are proposed descriptors, not findings

Part III supplies six families that can index a history without becoming diagnoses:

| Family | Place in the longitudinal record | Discriminating question |
|---|---|---|
| global redistribution | configuration and distribution across several regions or contacts | Which declared regional contribution changed, and did a local exposure change with it? |
| local concentration | distribution-to-local-exposure arrow | Is a named field more focused at the receiver than under the comparator? |
| segmental instability | repeated configuration, departure, and correction through time | Did variability, correction, contact, or control demand change beyond measurement noise? |
| timing or handoff failure | transfer among carriers or phases | Was demand transferred late, early, abruptly, incompletely, or by another carrier? |
| constraint-maintained deformation or lost excursion | persistent boundary condition and receiver state | Does the deformation or excursion change when the maintaining relation changes? |
| compensatory rerouting | protected function followed through later tasks | What was preserved, what route changed, and did the later exposure differ? |

Scale transition describes movement among levels of analysis; it is not a seventh adverse family. Several families may coexist, none may apply, and a history may change categories with task or time.

The taxonomy has no established reliability or validity merely because its definitions are clear to its authors. Kottner and colleagues' GRRAS guidance identifies study design, rater and subject populations, measurement error and agreement, and statistical analysis as reporting obligations in reliability and agreement studies.[^kottner2011] A concrete comparator shows the risk: in one forty-five-person shoulder-pain study, two trained therapists applying a movement-system-impairment classification achieved fair agreement for broad subgroups and an overall kappa of 0.11 for the set of subcategory judgments.[^torwichien2020] This result neither invalidates movement analysis nor predicts the reliability of the six families. It demonstrates why training, apparent face validity, and percentage agreement cannot substitute for an independent reliability program.

Before the taxonomy carries a research claim, studies should test:

- whether raters can identify each family from the same record;
- whether families can be distinguished from one another and from “none”;
- whether agreement survives different tasks, regions, populations, and evidence completeness;
- whether a family predicts a prespecified measurement or transition beyond ordinary variables; and
- whether disagreements expose useful ambiguity or merely unstable definitions.

## Cumulative demand does not imply delayed failure

The protected project hypothesis is conditional:

```text
changed task or available path
    -> useful compensation or redistribution
    -> changed next exposure or starting state
    -> possible change in reserve, local exposure, or recovery demand
    -> possible later response, finding, symptom, or no consequence
```

Recovery, adaptation, external support, skill, environmental redesign, biological change, and ordinary variability can interrupt or reverse every arrow. A compensation can remain the best durable organization for a person's goals. A later finding can cause the compensation. A common upstream factor can change both. The absence of a discrete injury does not establish that cumulative injury occurred, and a nonspecific image cannot validate the missing history.

Chapter 18's contribution is to preserve these branches in a dated record. Chapter 19 must independently establish any exposure-to-tissue-response relation. Chapter 21 must evaluate causal priority and whole-person alternatives. The record itself does not turn delay into mechanism.

## A shared object does not create a new owner

Different contributors legitimately know different portions of a history:

| Contributor | Strongest likely contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| person, family, or support network | goals, felt changes, tasks, assistance, access, chronology, participation | report is not a directly measured internal route |
| biomechanics | kinematics, kinetics, models, contact, local estimates, sensitivity | a model is bounded by its inputs and validation domain |
| rehabilitation | function, capacity, movement, adaptation, repeated response, assistive context | movement response does not by itself establish diagnosis or etiology |
| ergonomics and occupational inquiry | work system, exposure duration, tools, organization, environment, feasible redesign | group or job estimates may not identify individual exposure |
| medical specialties | tissue state, diagnosis, exclusion, local endpoint, treatment history, prognosis | current endpoint may not reconstruct its entire history |
| psychology and social or developmental inquiry | attention, expectation, behavior, learning, stress, opportunity, constraints, life course | contextual contribution should enter a named node, not erase mechanics |

The routing record is a shared representation, not a professional jurisdiction. Conflicting observations remain visible. Chapter 22 owns the question of how much of this information belongs in an encounter; Chapter 18 only defines a research object.

## Incremental value is the survival test

The framework should be evaluated in layers:

1. **Feasibility:** Can the required fields be collected at an acceptable burden?
2. **Reliability and agreement:** Can the same record produce stable variables and classifications under declared conditions?
3. **Construct and discriminant validity:** Do proposed route variables correspond to appropriate direct or model-based comparators and distinguish the histories they claim to distinguish?
4. **Incremental explanation or prediction:** Do routing variables improve a prespecified outcome beyond an existing history, ergonomic, biomechanical, rehabilitation, or medical model?
5. **Communication or research value:** Does the shared representation reduce ambiguity, reveal missing measures, or coordinate a study better than ordinary documentation?
6. **Decision value:** Where a decision is proposed, do benefits exceed error, burden, false positives, and opportunity cost?

Vickers and Elkin developed decision-curve analysis because predictive accuracy alone does not encode the consequences of decisions; their method compares net benefit across thresholds.[^vickers2006] Chapter 18 does not prescribe that method for every study. It inherits the principle that a model can fit or predict and still fail to help a decision. Explanatory, communication, and research uses need their own declared endpoints rather than borrowing “clinical utility.”

The no-added-value branches are substantive:

- a documented acute event and local endpoint already answer the immediate question;
- routing fields cannot be collected or classified reliably;
- several candidate histories remain observationally indistinguishable;
- the added variables do not improve explanation or prediction against the comparator;
- a mature ergonomic, biomechanical, rehabilitation, or medical framework already performs the same job;
- the record changes neither research design nor communication nor decision;
- collection burden excludes relevant populations or distorts behavior; or
- the larger story increases confidence without increasing evidence.

A negative result narrows the program. It does not deny that mechanical exposure exists.

## Primary reader-facing comparison

Return to the neutral endpoint with four records:

| Record | What is known | What remains unresolved | Immediate value of wider routing |
|---|---|---|---|
| documented acute event | event, timing, local endpoint, immediate function | later compensation and recovery | low for immediate local management; possibly useful later |
| repeated task with prospective sampling | task changes, selected configurations, external demand, repeated response | internal receiver and biological bridge may remain modeled | potentially useful for exposure and recovery research |
| retrospective cumulative account | reports, work records, selected models, present endpoint | missing intervals, recall, nonunique routes, reverse direction | hypothesis generation only unless discriminating evidence exists |
| biology/context-first candidate | changing capacity or context precedes mechanical change | relative contribution and feedback | prevents mechanical history from becoming assumed primary |

No row is the default winner. The record should conclude with the next discriminating observation, not with the most satisfying narrative.

## Chapter seams and reader-prose decisions

- **Chapter 17 owns** the fact that medicine already reasons mechanically. Chapter 18 begins with overlap and defines only the candidate increment.
- **Chapters 10–16 own** the six-family mechanics. Chapter 18 crosswalks them briefly rather than reteaching them.
- **Chapter 19 owns** local exposure to tissue response. This packet stops at a defined exposure-history node.
- **Chapter 21 owns** causal ranking and whole-person alternatives. Chapter 18 keeps candidate histories and reverse direction visible.
- **Chapter 22 owns** workflow. No intake form, examination sequence, routing score, or professional role is created here.
- **Chapters 23–26 own** condition-specific narratives. The primary comparison here remains neutral and hypothetical.
- **Book II owns** preferred configuration. It may later generate route hypotheses, but Chapter 18's record does not depend on one.

The reader-facing chapter should translate evidence-state abbreviations into plain language after introducing the distinction once. It should not read like a data dictionary. Its positive arc is: existing fields already measure history; a shared cross-scale routing record may add an inspectable relation; reconstruction is bounded; prospective measurement can improve selected links; the proposal survives only by beating a comparator.
