## Closure boundary

This record closes the internal Wave 1 integration decision required by the [execution sequence](../planning/research-and-salvage-execution-sequence.md). It reconciles the three accepted [chapter briefs](../chapter-briefs/), focused [evidence packs](../evidence/), locator-level [salvage sheets](../salvage/), and shared [claim](../claim-evidence-ledger.md), [model](../model-registry.md), [example](../example-registry.md), and [figure](../figure-registry.md) controls.

The Wave 1 exit condition is satisfied by a bounded route: material whose publication review remains open is either labeled and limited in the first draft or excluded from load-bearing prose. Therefore:

- **authorized** means it may perform its stated job in a controlled first draft;
- **limited** means it may appear only with the stated posture, provenance, and negative boundary;
- **deferred** means its conceptual place is preserved but it cannot carry this draft;
- **quarantined** means the expression, equation, or generated passage cannot be promoted, although its underlying question may remain; and
- **publication-gated** means the first draft may proceed, but final wording or art cannot be approved until the named review is complete.

No external mechanics, biomechanics, anatomy, ultrasound-method, vascular, medical, visual, rights, or accessibility review is represented here as completed. First-draft authorization does not change any claim's posture or evidence prior, any model's lifecycle state, any example's selection state, or any figure's `specified` state.
