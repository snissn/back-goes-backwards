## Recovery decision

The archive contains a much larger proposal than a documentation aid. It imagines biomechanical reasoning becoming a consequential part of prevention, diagnosis, research, education, and care; it calls for new measurements, validated assessment tools, cross-disciplinary collaboration, environmental redesign, and eventually practical interventions. That medical-reform ambition is direct project intent. It must remain visible as an active development program rather than being rewritten into a permanent promise that mechanics can only be a modest adjunct to existing work.

The same archive also moves too quickly from a compelling mechanical picture to diagnostic authority, clinical benefit, or a prescribed correction. It sometimes treats hard/soft routing, alignment, posterior participation, symptom modulation, or a reconstructed compensation history as if any one of them could already identify the root cause of a person's concern. The later Part III specification recognized this problem and introduced mechanism-first organization, tiered claims, explicit exclusions, skeptic handling, and a no-protocol boundary.

Chapter 22 should preserve both developments. Its present contribution is not to renounce the larger ambition and not to deploy it ahead of validation. It should create the interface through which a more ambitious mechanical medicine could become inspectable:

> Begin with the person's clinical problem, safety and diagnostic context, current care, goals, and professional scope. Add load history and routing only for a declared question. Preserve the source and evidence state of every consequential entry, compare alternative structures, and stop when the added account cannot discriminate or changes nothing.

This is a conceptual encounter sequence and an editorial research record. It is additive at the current publication layer because its reliability, validity, incremental value, utility, and safety have not yet been established. “Additive” does not settle the eventual importance of the model. Later evidence could show duplication, a narrow documentation benefit, a useful cross-disciplinary representation, a condition-specific clinical contribution, or grounds for more substantial reform.

The archive's clinical content should therefore be recovered in four layers:

1. **Book I direct content:** clinical context first; selective task and load history; provenance; modulation-versus-origin; bounded findings; overlap with established professions; alternatives; stopping rules; and a common record for the Part V narratives.
2. **Book I reconstruction:** hard/soft routing, silent compensation, delayed consequence, imaging lag, and system-wide mechanical history become specific, evidence-state-labeled questions rather than diagnoses.
3. **Protected development program:** measurement, validation, professional tools, education, environmental design, prevention, and possible changes to medical reasoning remain active in Chapters 27–29 and Book V.
4. **Reversible quarantine:** inherited diagnostic frameworks, home tests, referral rules, protocols, posterior/breath corrections, and outcome promises remain recoverable but cannot enter this chapter as present authority.

Nothing substantive is deleted by this allocation. The chapter protects the future program by making its current state clear enough to evaluate.

## Disposition legend

- **D — direct recovery:** the concept can enter Chapter 22 substantially as intended.
- **R — reconstruct:** preserve the intended question while separating variables, chronology, causal arrows, evidence states, or professional roles.
- **P — protect forward:** retain the concept for Chapters 27–29, Book V, a professional-development program, or later condition-specific research.

## Source-obligation reconciliation

### BGB-S-1125 — a larger role for biomechanics within medicine

**Preserved:** The project intends mechanical history and routing to matter before and beyond a rehabilitation technique. Candidate contributions include better questions about exposure, chronology, reserve, recovery, symptom context, measurement, prevention, environmental design, education, and the relation among diagnosis, medication, procedures, surgery, rehabilitation, work, and daily function.

**Current allocation:** Chapter 22 shows how one bounded mechanical question could enter an existing care account without acquiring clinical authority merely because it is coherent. It makes the larger proposal evaluable through comparators, provenance, discriminators, burden, stopping rules, and no-added-value results.

**Protected future:** If research establishes reliable observations, valid constructs, discrimination, incremental utility, acceptable burden, and favorable outcomes, later work may justify a larger change in medical education or care. The archive's reform ambition is not reduced to permanent adjunct status.

### BGB-S-1220 — hard/soft routing as a clinical lens

**Preserved:** Hard/soft can ask whether gross demand is being resolved through capable shape-preserving support or whether form-receiving structures are being required to sustain a deformation, substitute for support, or carry a kind or dose of demand relevant to the declared question. The polarity is relational, task-, scale-, direction-, and state-dependent.

**Provenance:** The distinction is motivated by TCM hard/soft and Yin/Yang concepts but is not definitionally identical to a traditional taxonomy. Traditional provenance neither validates nor disqualifies the mechanical question.

**Reconstruction:** Hard/soft is not a diagnosis, tissue classification, score, required encounter field, or proof of one correct route. A bone can deform; a muscle can shape-preserve; a vessel can bear pressure; and a structure may change role across direction, phase, task, or condition. Any consequential use must name the receiver, quantity, sustaining relation, and evidence state.

### BGB-S-1225 — limits, referral, and conventional-care integration

**Preserved:** The archive recognized that a mechanical model must coexist with diagnosis, medication, surgery, rehabilitation, specialty care, behavioral and occupational work, and the person's goals. It also recognized contexts in which a mechanical account is incomplete, secondary, unsafe as a stand-alone approach, or not presently useful.

**Current allocation:** Chapter 22 preserves safety and care context as logical prerequisites and cites applicable professional standards rather than composing a new red-flag, contraindication, referral, or treatment system. Chapter 21 owns causal scope; Chapter 22 owns the interface and stop branch; Book V owns any future application artifact after evidence, safety, scope, and governance review.

### BGB-S-1302 — no protocols in the foundational medical argument

**Preserved:** Book I develops a way to reason, compare, record, and test. It does not tell a reader or clinician how to diagnose a routing problem, correct it, alter care, or decide referral.

**Protected rather than erased:** The archive's tests, cues, corrections, movement programs, breath practices, and professional tools remain part of the series' application program. They move to Book V with construct, audience, reliability, validity, adverse-event, contraindication, referral, scope, and outcome obligations. Their absence from Chapter 22 is a development-stage boundary, not a decision that applications can never exist.

### BGB-S-1303 — answer “just physical therapy” and “alternative medicine”

**Preserved:** Both objections identify real design tests. Physical therapy, rehabilitation, biomechanics, ergonomics, occupational therapy, surgery, pain care, and other disciplines already address movement, load, capacity, task, environment, and change. The project must compare rather than caricature that work. Traditional motivation and embodied observation must retain provenance without substituting for mechanical or clinical evidence.

**Reconstruction:** “Just physical therapy” can resolve to full duplication, useful vocabulary, a clearer longitudinal research object, a genuinely distinct measured relation, or an unvalidated hypothesis. “Alternative medicine” is answered by the project's evidentiary method and care interface, not by denying its traditional sources or inventing a new professional identity.

### BGB-S-1311 — silent compensation, delayed consequence, and imaging lag

**Preserved:** A person may maintain task success while changing strategy, effort, distribution, variability, reserve, or recovery. Consequential exposure and symptoms may be separated in time or location, and a selected image may fail to capture a dynamic relation or the relevant historical interval.

**Reconstruction:** “Silent” means that a candidate change was not captured by the selected outcome, not that hidden damage is presumed. Delayed or distal consequence requires a dated chain and receiver-specific bridges. Imaging can constrain anatomy or show tissue state, but neither a finding nor its absence supplies the whole history. A reconstructed compensation remains an inference unless it was observed or measured at the needed times and scales.

### BGB-S-1313 — routing as context, not diagnosis

**Preserved:** A routing account may organize task, chronology, mechanical exposure, response, findings, symptoms, function, and care without becoming a disease label. Medication, surgery, devices, rehabilitation, work changes, and other interventions can enter the history as dated changes to biology, anatomy, environment, demand, or capacity.

**Current output:** The chapter produces a bounded interpretation: the declared question, supported present relations, proposed arrows, alternatives, missing cells, possible discriminators, care boundary, and whether the representation added useful information. It produces no diagnosis, prognosis, referral decision, or treatment selection.

### BGB-S-1315 — explicit skeptic handling

**Preserved:** Strong objections belong within the design. A coherent mechanical story is not necessarily identifiable; present modulation does not reveal historical origin; an image can be real but nondiscriminating; first-person evidence can be important without measuring internal force; more fields can add burden rather than value; and an established field may already do the proposed work.

**Reconstruction:** The skeptic is not answered with reassurance or authorial certainty. Each objection becomes a comparator, missing observation, alternative graph, burden measure, disfavoring result, or stopping condition.

All eight assigned source obligations are directly represented, reconstructed, protected, or explicitly quarantined. No intended concept is silently discarded.

## Structural recovery from the archive

The archive contributes twelve durable moves to Chapter 22:

1. **Mechanical reasoning may be medically upstream.** It can shape questions about exposure, chronology, reserve, prevention, measurement, and interpretation before a rehabilitation plan exists.
2. **The encounter does not begin with the project model.** The person's problem, safety and diagnostic context, current care, goals, and professional scope define the question.
3. **History is selective.** Task, environment, demand, repetition, recovery, compensation, and context enter only when they perform work for the declared question.
4. **Provenance precedes causal synthesis.** Reports, observations, measurements, calculations, models, inferences, hypotheses, embodied observations, and reconstructions retain distinct jobs.
5. **Hard/soft remains available as a question.** It can organize possible shape-preserving and form-receiving roles without becoming a tissue taxonomy or diagnostic test.
6. **Present modulation does not prove origin.** A change in symptom, function, or measured output can be real and useful while the historical cause remains open.
7. **Findings occupy bounded nodes.** Imaging, examination, laboratory, and functional findings require condition- and question-specific correspondence.
8. **Care changes the mechanical history.** Medication, procedures, surgery, devices, rehabilitation, work, and environment can alter boundary conditions without being reduced to mechanics.
9. **Overlap is a resource and a test.** Existing disciplines provide methods, comparators, collaborators, and the possibility that the proposed representation adds little.
10. **A stop is a successful output.** Nonuniqueness, missing discrimination, higher-priority context, duplication, burden, or no decision relevance can end the inquiry.
11. **Worked narratives need a common record.** Chapters 23–26 must expose every consequential node, arrow, alternative, null, missing cell, evidence state, provenance source, and boundary.
12. **The larger program remains contingent and alive.** Research may support a narrow representational benefit or justify more substantial reform; Book I does not predetermine the result.

## Clean conceptual recovery

### Preserve directly

- Begin with the clinical problem, safety context, known or considered diagnosis, current care, function, goals, preferences, and scope.
- Add task, environment, load history, compensation, variability, and recovery only for a named question.
- Keep direct report, observation, measurement, calculation, model, inference, hypothesis, embodied observation, and reconstructed history distinguishable.
- Let a patient's experience remain legitimate evidence for the experience and its relevance without literalizing it as tissue stress or a unique route.
- Treat symptom or function modulation as a present relation under stated conditions, not automatic proof of etiology.
- Place imaging and other findings at the node and construct they actually measure.
- Represent diagnosis, medication, surgery, procedures, devices, rehabilitation, physical therapy, ergonomics, occupational work, psychology, family, and environment fairly.
- Compare the proposed routing record with existing practice rather than declaring novelty.
- Preserve biological, psychological, social, occupational, environmental, developmental, care, and meaning relations at named nodes.
- Make low mechanical priority, underdetermination, no added value, and stop legitimate results.
- Use a minimum common record to make Part V examples comparable and auditable.
- Preserve the possibility that successful later evidence could justify substantial medical and educational change.

### Reconstruct rather than erase

- “Biomechanics belongs at the foundation of medicine” becomes a program to test whether explicit mechanical history and routing improve defined questions, measurements, communication, decisions, or outcomes.
- “Hard-to-soft failure reveals the root cause” becomes a receiver-specific question about shape-preserving support, form-receiving deformation, sustaining relations, dose, response, and alternatives.
- “Silent compensation caused delayed degeneration” becomes a dated candidate chain in which task success, changed strategy, exposure, response, finding, symptom, and function remain separate.
- “Imaging lagged the mechanism” becomes a comparison between what the selected imaging construct can show and the dynamic or historical relation being proposed.
- “A position or support improved the problem” becomes an observed or measured present modulation with expectation, learning, fluctuation, concurrent care, and other mechanisms retained.
- “This is beyond physical therapy” becomes an explicit comparator asking what chronology, scale, receiver, vocabulary, or research utility the new representation adds, if anything.
- “Conventional medicine must be complemented” becomes an open evidence-governed range from duplication through coordination to substantive revision.
- “Develop clinical tools” becomes a staged construct, content, feasibility, reliability, validity, discrimination, utility, equity, safety, and governance program.
- “Use biomechanical screening in routine care” becomes a future application hypothesis requiring separate clinical-instrument development rather than an implied consequence of this chapter.

- validated longitudinal mechanical-history and routing representations;
- professional observation, assessment, screening, communication, and decision-support tools;
- condition-specific mechanical contribution, prediction, prevention, and treatment hypotheses;
- measurement systems joining task, force, motion, pressure, imaging, biomarkers, and patient-reported outcomes;
- environmental and occupational redesign informed by mechanical exposure;
- changes to medical, rehabilitation, movement, and public education;

- preferred-configuration and posterior-necessity hypotheses in Book II;

## Reader-facing sequence

1. **Open in an ordinary encounter.** Establish the person's concern, function, current care, goals, and applicable clinical context before introducing the project lens.
2. **Declare one mechanical question.** Ask whether task, exposure, chronology, current modulation, recovery, or another bounded relation needs clarification.
3. **Add only relevant history.** Record task, environment, demand, repetition, variability, change, compensation, support, recovery, and context to the depth the question requires.
4. **Disclose evidence state and provenance.** Keep report, observation, measurement, calculation, model, inference, hypothesis, embodied observation, and reconstruction distinguishable.
5. **Introduce hard/soft only as an optional query.** Explain shape-preserving and form-receiving roles, their TCM motivation, and the need to name receiver and sustaining relation.
6. **Interpret present modulation narrowly.** Show how a real change can inform the present without recovering historical origin.
7. **Place findings at their proper nodes.** Let imaging, examination, laboratory, and functional results constrain the account without carrying the whole chain.
8. **Map the care interface.** Show how diagnosis, medication, surgery, procedures, rehabilitation, physical therapy, ergonomics, occupational work, pain care, and the person's environment change or inform the history.
9. **Answer the professional objections empirically.** Compare the routing record with existing practice and disclose traditional provenance without using it as clinical proof.
10. **Produce a bounded interpretation.** State the supported relation, proposed arrows, alternatives, missing observations, weakest consequential link, and possible discriminator.
11. **Run the stop branch.** Show a complete result in which the added lens is nonunique, redundant, burdensome, lower priority, or irrelevant to the declared question.
12. **Build the Part V handoff.** Convert the encounter into the common record used by Chapters 23–26, including blanks and nulls rather than smoothing them into a story.
13. **Close with three possible futures.** The representation may clarify a present relation, produce a researchable but incomplete history, or add no value; later validation determines whether the broader medical program grows.

The neutral composite should be source-grounded and nondramatic. It must not use thoracic outlet as its primary case, invent a rescue by posture, or end with an implied recommendation.

## Draft-audit obligations

The later first-draft audit must verify that:

- the opening establishes the person's concern, applicable clinical context, current care, function, goals, preferences, and scope before the added lens;
- the workflow is repeatedly identifiable as conceptual and unvalidated without defensive clutter;
- the chapter asks one declared question rather than constructing an exhaustive parallel assessment;
- reports, observations, measurements, calculations, models, inferences, hypotheses, embodied observations, and reconstructions retain provenance;
- reconstructed is treated as a temporal qualifier, not a promotion in evidence status;
- hard/soft is described as shape-preserving/form-receiving, motivated by TCM hard/soft and Yin/Yang concepts but not definitionally identical;
- hard/soft is optional, relational, receiver-specific, and never a diagnosis, score, or fixed tissue class;
- symptom or function modulation is not used to establish historical origin, preferred configuration, or a universal correction;
- imaging and other findings are condition- and question-specific and are neither causal destiny nor dismissed as irrelevant;
- compensation, delay, distal consequence, and imaging mismatch remain candidate histories with dated arrows and missing evidence;
- diagnosis, medication, surgery, procedures, rehabilitation, physical therapy, ergonomics, occupational work, pain care, and whole-person context are represented fairly;
- the “just physical therapy” objection becomes an empirical comparison and not a novelty claim;
- traditional provenance is disclosed without borrowing clinical authority or being treated as disqualifying;
- an explicit no-added-value branch ends the mechanical inquiry without rhetorical failure;
- stopping conditions are validity rules for the representation, not patient advice or referral criteria;
- the Part V record exposes every consequential node, arrow, alternative, null, missing cell, evidence state, provenance entry, boundary, and destination;
- the larger medical-reform ambition remains visible as contingent future work and is not reduced to permanent adjunct status;
- no self-test, score, screen, diagnosis, prognosis, red-flag list, referral threshold, care sequence, intervention, drill, or protocol enters reader prose;
- no planning key, prompt, generated case, or archive certainty survives as manuscript authority;
- every citation in reader prose supports the exact population, construct, and conclusion assigned to it; and
- all figures satisfy model, medical, rights, caption, accessibility, and final-art gates.

## Authorization

This salvage packet and the companion evidence packet have completed the independent source-and-provenance gate for a Chapter 22 reader draft. No further authorial decision blocks the chapter architecture.

The authorized draft may:

- affirm that the project's ambition extends to possible reform of medical reasoning, education, prevention, and care;
- describe the current publication posture as additive, conceptual, and unvalidated without making that posture a permanent ceiling;
- begin from existing clinical context, current care, goals, preferences, and professional scope;
- add task and mechanical history selectively for one declared question;
- preserve the evidence state, source, time, task, and uncertainty of consequential entries;
- use hard/soft as an optional shape-preserving/form-receiving question with its TCM motivation disclosed;
- separate present modulation from historical origin and findings from symptoms, function, diagnosis, and outcome;
- map overlap and interfaces with established professions and care;
- include alternatives, missing observations, discriminators, burden, nulls, and stopping rules;
