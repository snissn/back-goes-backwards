## Causal and evidence-state audit

The chapter preserves the canonical sequence:

```text
task and environment
    -> configuration and available paths
    -> external demand and internal distribution
    -> local receiver exposure
    -> response through time
    -> function, finding, or symptom
    -> bounded interpretation or no-added-value result
```

No later node retrospectively proves an earlier one. In particular:

- task performance does not establish internal distribution;
- an observed movement does not become local stress or strain;
- a report or embodied experience does not become a measured force line;
- calculation and model output do not become direct observation;
- a present finding does not recover the exposure history;
- symptom or function modulation does not establish initiating cause;
- a mechanically motivated intervention does not guarantee its intended mediator or outcome;
- imaging does not become either causal destiny or irrelevant noise; and
- narrative completeness does not become diagnosis or clinical utility.

Reconstructed is used as a temporal qualifier, not an evidence rank. The prose states that dated observations can support a reconstruction while the unobserved interval and compatible histories remain open. Missing cells are treated as positive information rather than drafting defects.

Compensation is not defined as hidden damage. It is a possible preservation of task or outcome through changed strategy, effort, distribution, variability, reserve, or recovery, with successful, neutral, protective, costly, and incidental possibilities retained. Recovery is decomposed into symptom change, task performance, reversed deformation, and restored capacity rather than treated as one event.

Whole-person conditions enter specific nodes and arrows: work organization and caregiving can shape task and options; sleep and expectations can shape selected responses; disease, medication, surgery, disability, and current care can change tissue state, active control, anatomy, or the next boundary conditions. They are not appended as a generic list after a mechanical conclusion.

## Clinical interface and incremental-value audit

The chapter’s sequence is a reasoning boundary, not a patient or clinician procedure. Existing evaluation, care, and professional obligations remain logically prior without the book reproducing emergency criteria, referral rules, contraindications, or care steps.

Figure 22.3 and its surrounding prose present professional domains through overlap rather than territorial boxes. The proposed record may connect dated information while preserving the original construct and source. Diagnosis, imaging, medication, procedures, surgery, rehabilitation, physical therapy, occupational therapy, ergonomics, psychology, pain care, and person/family knowledge are not reduced to mechanics or subordinated to project vocabulary.

The physical-therapy comparison remains open to four outcomes: complete duplication, substantive existing work plus a useful vocabulary, a genuinely additional longitudinal or receiver-specific representation, or an unvalidated and presently unobservable hypothesis. The chapter proposes empirical comparison through agreement, discrimination, measurement, communication, burden, generalization, and meaningful consequence. It does not answer the objection through professional caricature.

The chapter also preserves the project’s reform ambition. Additive describes the current evidentiary and publication layer. Future reliability, validity, utility, safety, and outcome evidence could support a larger role; equally, explicit comparators could show that the representation adds little. Neither outcome is decided editorially.

## Hard/soft and traditional-provenance audit

Hard/soft appears once as an optional query rather than a required workflow field. The prose asks whether gross demand is resolved through relatively shape-preserving support or whether a form-receiving structure sustains deformation or substitutes for support in a way relevant to the declared question.

The polarity is explicitly motivated by traditional Chinese medicine’s hard/soft and Yin/Yang concepts but not treated as definitionally identical to them. Bone may deform, active muscle may act shape-preservingly, and a vessel may bear pressure while remaining form-receiving. Any consequential use must name the receiver, task, direction, quantity, sustaining relation, and evidence. Traditional provenance neither validates nor disqualifies the mechanical proposal.

No preferred configuration, posterior necessity, coordinate map, breath geometry, bandha, meridian, or corrective practice bears any part of the chapter’s current reasoning. Later programs appear only once, in the statement that a negative result for the present record does not automatically decide every later hypothesis and that those programs cannot inherit clinical utility from it.

## Stopping and development audit

The chapter separates construct definition, content, feasibility, reliability, validity, discrimination, incremental value, utility, outcomes, equity, burden, privacy, safety, and governance. Clear documentation is not treated as validation, reliability as validity, validity as usefulness, or prediction as beneficial decision consequence.

The comparator and outcome must be named before success is claimed. Existing documentation, rehabilitation history, ergonomic analysis, local biomechanical model, or no added record can each serve as a declared comparator. Possible outputs include communication, research design, agreement, discrimination, prediction, or decision consequence; the prose does not collapse them into a generic claim of usefulness.

Stopping rules apply only to whether the proposed representation should continue to occupy the narrative. They are not clinical referral criteria or patient advice. Nonuniqueness, inability to distinguish an internal route, a single vivid finding carrying the chain, higher-priority context, duplication, burden, and no meaningful change are all authorized stop conditions.
