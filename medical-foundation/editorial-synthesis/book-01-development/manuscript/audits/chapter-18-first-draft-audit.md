## Endpoint, chronology, and provenance audit

The opening's status is explicit: it is a hypothetical comparison rather than a patient, composite, or diagnostic exercise. The current endpoint is separated from the candidate histories that precede it. The four branches include a documented local event, repeated task and compensation, biology or context first, and a mixed or unknown interval. The prose states that compatible does not mean equally probable and gives each history a different discriminating observation.

The routing record is ordered forward through at least five times. Task and environment, available paths, dated change, distribution, local receiver, compensation or recovery, and present endpoint remain separate entries. Interventions and changing capacity can enter the chronology rather than disappearing inside an assumed uninterrupted mechanical sequence. Reverse direction is explicit: a later finding, pain, treatment, or contextual change can alter the mechanics observed now.

Source verbs retain evidentiary meaning. A task can be observed or reported; an external force can be measured; a moment can be calculated; an internal distribution can be modeled; an experience can be reported; and a missing interval can remain unknown. The draft does not use retrospective assembly as a synonym for prospective observation. It also does not treat a job-level estimate, current appearance, or embodied continuity as direct measurement of an individual internal route.

The principal quantity boundaries remain intact:

- task demand is not internal distribution;
- posture or configuration is not force;
- an external force or calculated moment is not local tissue stress or strain;
- repetition count, force–time integral, cumulative moment, vibration dose, modeled compression, and recovery interval are not one exposure variable;
- modeled exposure is not tissue response;
- current finding is not prior cause; and
- response to support is not proof of the route that initiated the current state.

The correct output can be a partial history. Unknown cells are treated as information about the research problem rather than permission to fill a route by anatomical plausibility.

## Existing-field overlap and novelty audit

The draft does not repeat Chapter 17's cardiopulmonary, fixation, decompression, or device-interface catalogue. It inherits the established common ground and moves to the distinct novelty question.

ICF is used for body-function, activity, participation, and environmental context, not as a routing or causal model. The International Ergonomics Association is used for established person–task–tool–organization–environment systems reasoning. The Rehabilitation Treatment Specification System supplies a bounded comparator that separates target, ingredient, mechanism, and later aim. Biomechanics, exposure science, rehabilitation, medicine, the person, and contextual disciplines retain their own legitimate objects.

The proposed increment is limited to an inspectable relation among selected observations across scale and time. It may preserve source differences and disagreement better than a fragmented account. It loses novelty or usefulness when an existing ergonomic, biomechanical, rehabilitation, exposure, or medical record already performs the job.

Candidate applications remain hypotheses. Exposure, recurrence, device or surgical follow-up, environmental design, recovery, functional reserve, prevention research, and ambiguous chronic histories each receive a comparator requirement. No application becomes a clinical tool, preventive rule, mechanical-primary conclusion, or professional jurisdiction.

## Cumulative exposure and measurement audit

The Norman, Seidler, and Bovenzi examples establish that peak, cumulative, reconstructed, and time-varying physical exposures are existing research objects. Their populations, designs, quantities, and limits remain in the body and notes. The case–control studies do not become individual causal recovery; the prospective vibration study does not become complete internal routing. Metric choice is treated as part of the result rather than a technical afterthought.

The Spielholz, d'Errico, and Korshøj examples make reconstruction uncertainty field-specific. Self-report does not become uniformly invalid, direct or observed measures do not become perfect ground truth for every variable, and a job-exposure matrix does not become an individual's measured exposure. Dates, aggregation, units, missing intervals, receiver, and model assumptions remain necessary disclosures.

The Greene, OpenCap, and Liang examples give prospective work a positive path without measurement maximalism. A two-dimensional method demonstration remains distinct from route validation. OpenCap's ten-person laboratory validation remains separate from its one-hundred-person field workflow. Smartphone capture retains plane-, task-, and metric-specific errors. The draft requires prospective designs to predeclare a small set of discriminating variables, relevant times, measurement error, comparators, and disfavoring results.

## Taxonomy, compensation, and delayed-consequence audit

The six adverse-organization families are used once as cross-time pattern language. Global or regional redistribution, local concentration, instability, timing or handoff failure, constraint-maintained deformation or lost excursion, and compensatory rerouting remain consistently named. Scale transition is not turned into a seventh family.

The GRRAS source is used only for reliability and agreement reporting obligations. The shoulder-classification study is a bounded example showing why face validity and training do not establish agreement; it is not transferred as evidence for or against this taxonomy. The draft specifies tests of rater agreement, discrimination from “none,” cross-task and cross-population stability, and incremental prediction. Revision or replacement remains a live outcome.

Compensation begins as a strategy that preserves function. The later history branches to resolution, durable useful organization, changed exposure, adequate recovery, reverse direction, unrelated outcome, and no meaningful consequence. Functional reserve is operationalized only for a declared task as ability to vary, repeat, answer perturbation, or recover over a stated interval. It is not a hidden global scalar.

The negative project hypothesis survives without becoming inevitable decline. Repetition can change the next exposure, and a mechanically meaningful history can occur without a discrete injury. The draft equally states that absence of trauma does not establish cumulative injury, before-symptom chronology does not imply occult disease, and a later or nonspecific finding cannot validate an unobserved route.

## Shared-object and incremental-value audit

The chapter gives each contributor a bounded evidentiary role and preserves disagreement. The person supplies goals, chronology, experience, assistance, and participation. Biomechanics supplies measured and modeled quantities. Rehabilitation supplies function, capacity, adaptation, support, and repeated response. Ergonomics and occupational inquiry supply tasks, tools, duration, organization, and environment. Medicine supplies tissue states, diagnoses, exclusions, procedures, and local endpoints. Psychological, social, and developmental inquiry can change named nodes without erasing mechanics.

The incremental-value ladder keeps six achievements non-equivalent. Feasibility does not establish reliability. Reliability does not establish validity. Validity does not establish incremental explanation or prediction. Prediction does not establish communication value or decision benefit. The Vickers and Elkin source supports only the final distinction between model performance and decision consequences; it is not presented as a required method or validation of routing.

The no-added-value example is concrete: a documented local event and adequate endpoint record can answer the immediate question without a wider reconstruction. Further stopping branches include unacceptable burden, unstable classification, observationally indistinguishable histories, duplication of mature practice, no improvement in a prespecified result, and confidence increasing without evidence. Negative outcomes narrow the program rather than being reinterpreted as invisible routing.

## Internal decision and next action

Chapter 18 passes its controlled first-draft gate. It makes the project's proposed addition concrete without apologizing for it: a longitudinal routing record can relate task, environment, paths, distribution, local exposure, compensation, recovery, and later state while preserving how each entry became known. That object is more specific than a request to consider context and more disciplined than a recovered hidden cause.

Its distinct contribution is the survival test. The record must be feasible, reliable, valid for its declared construct, and incrementally useful relative to a named existing account. It can succeed as an explanatory, predictive, communication, research, or decision object only at the level actually tested. Duplication, nonuniqueness, burden, unrecoverable history, and no added value remain affirmative results.

The chapter is ready for controlled Part IV integration. It is not publication-approved. Chapter 19 may now begin from one bounded local-exposure history and ask what a living receiver does next, without inheriting a presumed tissue response, symptom, diagnosis, or outcome.
