## Executive result

The draft performs Chapter 27's authorized synthesis job. It converts Book I's reasoning method into exactly seven questions while denying the sequence the status of a score, screen, diagnostic method, treatment selector, self-test, or protocol. The questions proceed from task and system boundary to configuration and available paths; relational hard/soft roles; candidate local exposure; what a changed strategy preserved, if anything; modulation across task, support, time, and state; and measurement, uncertainty, and legitimate use.

The ordering exposes missing causal arrows rather than manufacturing continuity. Every proposed transition can support, weaken, redirect, remain unresolved, or stop. Confidence remains attached to a specific proposition, not averaged across a narrative. The draft permits a well-recorded task history with unknown internal distribution, a measured local exposure with uncertain clinical meaning, reproducible modulation with unresolved origin, and a mechanically coherent story with no consequential added value.

Compensation is earned rather than presumed. The chapter requires a changed condition or state, an altered strategy, and something the change preserved. It places protection before possible cost and preserves useful adaptation, no demonstrated cost, unresolved tradeoff, reverse causation, shared cause, incidental difference, and no established compensation as legitimate outcomes.

The recurring object transfer receives one compact callback. It is used only to show that changing a boundary changes force accounting, that low acceleration is not zero force, and that a net joint resultant does not uniquely identify tissue forces. No preferred transfer technique is depicted or implied.

The Part V comparison preserves four different evidentiary endings. Chapter 23 carries a strong bounded geometry-to-local-field relation while downstream biology and clinical meaning remain open. Chapter 24 carries co-observed focal anatomy and current arterial consequences while the repeated-deformation history remains reconstructed. Chapter 25 separates passive, task, experiential, and state-transition evidence. Chapter 26 is explicitly a transparent hypothetical method construction with no empirical case or established regional carrier.

Four original text-native figures make the boundary change, seven-question sequence and equal-weight stops, observation-to-use ladder, and Part V comparison visible. Fifteen chapter-scoped endnotes support source-dependent propositions. No reader-facing development identifiers, prompts, archive locators, migration notes, generated certainty, diagnostic instruction, correction rule, preferred configuration, or later-book mechanism carries the chapter.

## Scope and authority audit

Its current answer is a structured context for inquiry. It can organize observations, assumptions, alternatives, and unknowns. The chapter remains subordinate to ordinary clinical and professional responsibilities established in Chapters 21 and 22. It does not authorize conversion of the questions into a form, checklist, score, electronic record, screen, examination sequence, patient exercise, or teaching tool used to make clinical decisions.

## Causal-chain and evidence-state audit

The chapter preserves the shared sequence:

```text
task and environment
    -> configuration and available paths
    -> external demand and internal distribution
    -> local receiver exposure
    -> response through time
    -> finding, function, or symptom
    -> bounded interpretation or outcome
```

It does not treat a later node as proof of an earlier one. Visible configuration does not become internal stress. A segment resultant does not become a unique muscle or tissue force. Deformation does not become harm. Modulation does not recover initiation. Continued performance does not establish successful compensation, and a symptom does not reconstruct its load history.

Evidence objects remain distinct in reader prose: ordinary observation, embodied report, operational measure, clinical or research assessment, calculation, model output, inference, reconstruction, and hypothesis. Reconstruction is used as a temporal statement, not an evidence rank. The reader is told that each smaller system boundary creates a new demand question and that recursion should stop when it merely adds anatomy without discriminating among accounts.

The hard/soft treatment conforms to the Book I contract. Hard is relatively shape-preserving or structurally resolving; soft is relatively form-receiving through yielding, conforming, accommodating, containing, or glide. Both roles remain loaded and active. Bone, muscle, ligament, cartilage, vessel, external support, and other structures can take different roles at different scales and phases. Chapter 3 owns the full traditional provenance, so Chapter 27 does not restate it or imply definitional identity.

Posterior participation is present only as one task-specific candidate among anterior, lateral, rotational, pressure-mediated, and externally supported contributions. It is not expected, diagnosed, ranked, or corrected. Capacity appears as a changing relation among demand, receiver state, adaptation, illness, injury, fatigue, learning, care, variation, and recovery rather than a scalar reserve score.

## Compensation, alternatives, and stopping audit

The chapter applies the stronger packet rule that compensation must be earned. It separates:

- a difference from a changed strategy;
- a changed strategy from a compensation;
- preserved task or function from restored capacity;
- protection from cost;
- increased effort from demonstrated adverse consequence; and
- a mechanically plausible chronology from an observed causal history.

The confirmation-bias sources are used narrowly to justify recording observations and alternatives before assimilating them to a preferred route. They are not used to estimate bias prevalence in biomechanics or to characterize clinicians generally.

Stopping conditions are substantive and arrow-specific. The chapter permits a stop for missing task or boundary, non-operational observation, a drawn but unmeasured path, nonunique internal sharing, unnamed receiver or quantity, missing response chronology, excessive error, invalid or nonspecific interpretation, failure to distinguish alternatives, no incremental value, another higher-priority concern, role or scope limits, burden, anxiety, inequity, opportunity cost, and replacement by a validated domain method.

A stop applies to the declared proposition and use. It does not prove mechanics absent or irrelevant in every scale, task, or outcome. Conversely, a coherent early mechanical relation cannot keep advancing merely because a later arrow is difficult to observe.
