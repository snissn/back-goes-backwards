## Preferred-configuration and efficiency audit

The reader-facing hypothesis matches the authorized formulation: human anatomy may possess a preferred body-wide organization associated with a real but still undefined whole-system efficiency relation, in which skeletal, tensile, active, and pressure-mediated support resolve gross demand while form-receiving structures retain relevant freedoms.

Preferred is not equated with a frozen pose, visual straightness, symmetry, comfort, frequency, low activation, statistical normality, or health. The draft requires a system, task, population, state, comparator, period, and criterion. It preserves several possible mathematical objects—a configuration family, task-dependent region, phase-dependent bias, routing regime, or return tendency—without claiming that any is already formalized.

Efficiency remains open among strain distribution, active or metabolic cost, stability, resilience, reserve, adaptability, repeatability, preserved excursion, and return behavior. The prose makes tradeoffs explicit and routes the choice among a scalar objective, multi-objective relation, Pareto family, viability condition, attractor property, or replacement vocabulary to Book II.

Posterior participation receives its intended status: **the author's current expectation is necessary but insufficient within the eventual model domain**. The text defines both words, says the expectation is not derived from the Book I posterior evidence, and requires the next book to specify participation, quantity, scale, phase, domain, counterexamples, and disfavoring observations.

## Generalized-mode and nonidentity audit

The signed candidate is stated exactly:

- downward axial resolution as Yang and upward resolution as Yin;
- external or opening rotation as Yang and internal or closing organization as Yin; and
- outward expansion as Yang and inward contraction as Yin.

The chapter describes the proposal as two translation-like or shape-like relations plus one rotation-like relation. It does not call the modes a Cartesian vector basis, material-symmetry axes, principal-stress axes, or three force components. It distinguishes generalized coordinate from conjugate generalized load and explicitly separates configuration, velocity, acceleration, external action, internal load share, and return tendency.

Left/right and local/global transformation are open Book II obligations. The outward directions on the left and right are allowed to oppose in a room-fixed frame while sharing one bilateral expansive sign. Curved anatomy, pelvis, scapula, limb, changing contact, open and closed chains, coupling, respiratory phase, and task phase remain unresolved rather than silently assigned.

Orthotropy is correctly demoted to an optional local or regional constitutive approximation. It neither forces the generalized modes to exist nor establishes their preferred signs, and the reader draft does not call the whole body homogeneous or globally orthotropic.
