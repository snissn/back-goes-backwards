## Authorial-intent and hard/soft audit

The authorial proposition remains central rather than surviving only as a qualified aside. Its immediate translation preserves the intended division of labor:

- gross demand can be investigated in relation to comparatively shape-preserving and structurally resolving routes;
- form-receiving structures remain positively capable of yielding, conforming, gliding, containing, expanding, recoiling, and otherwise changing as their functions require;
- a soft role can carry and transmit substantial demand;
- force transmission alone does not assign a hard role;
- a hard role may deform substantially and can be performed by a nonmineralized or pressure-supported assembly;
- the candidate soft-to-hard anatomical sequence remains visible and testable rather than being deleted as an overclaim; and
- the negative maintained-substitution application remains owned by Chapters 11 and 13 rather than being prematurely developed here.

The prose uses **motivated by**, not *derived from* or *inspired by*. This follows direct author clarification and the accepted Chapter 3 controls where older contract and memo wording is stale. TCM motivation is disclosed early and revisited once at the assigned provenance seam. It never functions as mechanical evidence.

## Controlled-salvage and scope audit

The draft recovers the strongest conceptual structure from the `new` archive without inheriting its fixed taxonomy or causal escalation:

- integrated skeletal, active, tensile, compliant, contact, sliding, and pressure-mediated cooperation survives;
- the hard body as structural capacity survives through a relative role rather than a permanent anatomical compartment;
- the fascia/muscle → tendon/ligament → bone/joint sequence survives as a bounded candidate with graded, branched, parallel, and nonsequential alternatives;
- softness survives as form reception and shape conformation rather than weakness or absence of load;
- role exchange survives across direction, phase, activation, pressure, state, scale, and time;
- TCM and Yin/Yang motivation remains visible without an exact identity claim; and
- posterior, systemic, medical, breath, correspondence, and practice extensions remain protected for their assigned later units rather than erased.

The reader draft contains no fixed posterior-hard/anterior-soft coloring, universal route, engineered-body teleology, organ-protection claim, immune or chronic-disease cascade, diagnostic inference, treatment implication, home test, correction, movement cue, exercise, or protocol. Posterior anatomy appears only once as an explicitly unproved route identity. Six-Division, signed-mode, all-Yang, breath-driver, bandha, torus, and practice programs do not enter the argument.

## Internal decision and next action

Chapter 3 passes its controlled first-draft gate. It preserves the authorial hard/soft proposition generously, defines both roles positively and relationally, prevents tissue taxonomy and stiffness/compliance collapse, retains the candidate tissue handoff, presents ordinary high-load and pressure-mediated counterexamples, accepts discriminating tests and genuine falsifiers, and gives the traditional motivation a visible but non-load-bearing home.

The draft is ready for Chapter 3 → Chapter 4 continuity review and later Part I integration. It is not publication-approved. External specialist and authorial review may narrow examples or terminology without collapsing the chapter's valid job; a failure to demonstrate incremental value in selected systems would narrow hard/soft to a heuristic and provenance-bearing synthesis rather than justify rhetorical strengthening.
