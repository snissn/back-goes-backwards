# Front matter and Part-transition first-draft audit

**Material:** Book I introduction; openings for Parts I–VI; syntheses for Parts I–V

**Audit date:** 2026-07-11

**Decision:** Passes the internally controlled first-draft gate; whole-book and publication gates remain open

## Executive result

The introduction and Part transitions now give Book I a continuous reader-facing argument rather than leaving thirty chapters as an editorial sequence. The introduction states the durable thesis, defines longitudinal load routing, introduces hard/soft as relational shape-preserving and form-receiving roles motivated by traditional Chinese medicine, makes the affirmative but bounded posterior case, and discloses the later preferred-configuration program without making Book I depend on it.

The six Part openings state why each group of chapters exists and how its internal order advances the argument. The first five Part syntheses consolidate what has been earned, refuse the most likely overextensions, and create the next Part's question. Chapter 30 is intentionally reserved to close both Part VI and the book; a separate Part VI synthesis would duplicate or weaken that ending.

All files are clean reader prose. They contain no project IDs, prompt language, acceptance criteria, TODOs, generator instructions, or publication workflow notes except the introduction's reader-relevant disclosure that specialist, medical-coauthor, traditional-language, accessibility, rights, bibliography, and final-art review remain necessary.

## Narrative architecture

The transitions preserve the intended six-stage movement:

1. **Part I — Mechanical foundations of living structure:** moves from embodied mechanics to quantities, relational mechanical roles, configuration, and bounded load paths.
2. **Part II — The architecture of support:** maps cooperative anatomy before making the substantial but nonexclusive posterior case.
3. **Part III — Load management, failure, and compensation:** develops a taxonomy of adverse or protective mechanical organizations without turning them into diagnoses.
4. **Part IV — From mechanical organization to medical meaning:** locates the routing proposal inside mechanics already used by medicine and preserves every biological, causal, and whole-person bridge.
5. **Part V — Worked mechanical narratives:** tests the framework against four examples whose value includes negative, mixed, and unresolved results.
6. **Part VI — Reasoning, prevention, development, and the wider hypothesis:** separates questions that can be used now from educational, prevention, research, therapeutic, implementation, health-system, and later-series programs that require distinct validation.

The Part syntheses correctly hand off anatomy to failure grammar, failure grammar to medical meaning, medical meaning to worked narratives, and worked narratives to bounded development. No transition claims that the preceding Part established a preferred route, diagnosis, protocol, intervention, or medical overhaul.

## Foundational and later-series boundary

The introduction states the Book I thesis as a whole-person mechanical-history proposition. It expressly refuses mechanical exclusivity, one ideal posture, one universally correct movement, and one proven force path.

The title is handled on three levels that Chapter 30 will complete:

- posterior anatomy and function are real and substantial;
- posterior participation is a useful organizing lens whose contribution remains task- and quantity-specific; and
- a preferred body-wide organization with signed modes and necessary but insufficient posterior participation is a later hypothesis.

Failure of the stronger hypothesis is stated to leave the Book I foundation intact. The Part files preserve the same one-way dependency.

## Hard/soft language audit

Hard and soft are consistently roles, not permanent tissue classes. The reader-facing definition is:

- comparatively shape-preserving, path-establishing, constraining, or structurally resolving, while recognizing that force transmission alone assigns neither role; and
- comparatively form-receiving and shape-conforming, with the capacity to yield, glide, contain, expand, buffer, receive pressure, or change local shape.

Role assignment is always relative to direction, scale, phase, task, and relation. Softness is not weakness, hard is not bone alone, and the polarity is not an anterior/posterior moral ranking. The wording is **motivated by** traditional Chinese medicine; no file uses the rejected inspiration wording or claims definitional identity with traditional categories.

## Claim and clinical-boundary audit

The transitions preserve these stopping rules:

- external motion, joint resultants, tissue fields, and cellular response are different levels;
- visible posture does not reveal internal stress or strain;
- narrowing is distinguished from the relation that creates or maintains deformation;
- compensation is considered first for what it preserves;
- a regional route requires intermediate carriers, quantities, chronology, and a receiver;
- mechanical relevance can be initiating, localizing, amplifying, maintaining, modifying, consequential, interactive, incidental, or unresolved without requiring one premature universal taxonomy;
- mechanics remains alongside biological, psychological, social, developmental, environmental, and historical factors; and
- questions, education, prevention, measurement, intervention, implementation, and health-system change do not borrow validation from one another.

No front or Part file provides a diagnostic rule, self-test, corrective cue, movement drill, breathing practice, treatment choice, prognosis, screening threshold, workplace prescription, or claim that symptom modulation establishes origin.
