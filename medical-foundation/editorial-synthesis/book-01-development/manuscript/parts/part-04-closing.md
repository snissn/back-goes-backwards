## Part IV synthesis — Mechanical relevance without mechanical exclusivity

Part IV began by removing a false opposition. Medicine already uses mechanics, and a body-wide routing proposal need not compete with that expertise to add something useful. Its distinct object is the changing history of distribution: what carried demand, what altered the path, what exposure reached a receiver, what response followed, and what else could account for the same present state.

The Part also established how easily that object can be overstated. A recognized mechanical endpoint does not reveal its complete upstream route. A biologically plausible response does not prove the exposure occurred in a person. Similar words such as compression, tension, glide, and pressure do not make vessels, nerves, tendons, cartilage, and other compliant systems mechanically interchangeable. A symptom can change with task or support without identifying original cause. A diagnosis can constrain the account without supplying its entire history.

Mechanical relevance can therefore take several forms. It may help initiate a process, localize it, amplify it, maintain it, modify symptoms or function, result from another condition, interact with care, or remain incidental to the endpoint at hand. The role should be specified when it matters, but exploratory observations need not be forced prematurely into one universal causal taxonomy.

The clinical posture is additive and comparative. Established care retains its own indications and evidence. A routing account must show what information it adds, what decisions it changes, what errors it reduces, and what burdens or harms it could introduce. Until then, it remains a structured inquiry rather than a validated clinical instrument.

Part V now asks the framework to perform under pressure. Four narratives will trace complete candidate chains, preserve competing explanations, and expose the links that remain unknown. Their purpose is not to prove the book through examples. It is to show whether the book can remain useful when an example refuses a single clean answer.
