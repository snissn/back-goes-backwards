# Part VI — Reasoning, prevention, development, and the wider hypothesis

A framework can change inquiry before it changes care. That is the governing distinction of Part VI.

The lowest-risk use of the book is a better question: What demand entered the system? What configuration and paths were available? How were shape-preserving and form-receiving roles divided? Where, if anywhere, might a local exposure have accumulated? What, if anything, did a changed strategy preserve? What changes with task, support, time, or state? What can actually be measured, and where should the inference stop?

Chapter 27 develops those questions as a reasoning scaffold rather than a score, screen, or protocol. Chapter 28 asks what belongs in education, movement literacy, environmental design, and outcome-specific prevention research. It keeps comprehension, behavior, exposure, injury, symptom, participation, and system outcomes separate. Chapter 29 divides the larger development program into research, measurement, education, therapeutic development, implementation, and health-system work. The tracks can inform one another, but none advances merely because another produced an encouraging result.

This staging preserves the project's ambition. A present additive role for a routing lens is not a permanent claim that medicine can never be reorganized around better mechanical knowledge. It is the evidence posture appropriate before constructs, measurements, benefits, harms, professional roles, equity effects, burdens, and opportunity costs have been established.

Chapter 30 then closes the volume on three levels. The durable Book I foundation stands first. Posterior anatomy supplies a bounded organizing lens. The wider preferred-configuration program is stated directly as a hypothesis, with its current signed modes and posterior expectation, its destination in later books, and a rejection test that leaves the foundation intact.

The Part therefore moves from what can be asked now to what must be built next. It offers no corrective routine. Its endpoint is a researchable horizon: definitions clear enough to fail, measurements matched to their purposes, applications that do not borrow evidence from one another, and a series title whose strongest meaning is disclosed rather than assumed.
