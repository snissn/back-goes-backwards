# Book I Research and Controlled-Salvage Execution Sequence

**Status:** active execution control; Waves 0–1 closed for controlled first-draft use; Wave 7 is next

**Scope:** all 30 Book I units, from completed development briefs through research, controlled source salvage, reader-facing drafting, and publication review

## Purpose

The Book I architecture and chapter briefs are now detailed enough to support disciplined execution. The next phase is not a return to the old manuscript and not a single undifferentiated “research pass.” It is a sequence in which each chapter advances through explicit preparation, evidence, salvage, drafting, and review gates.

This plan turns the [Book I contract](../book-contract.md), 30 [chapter briefs](../chapter-briefs/), [claim-evidence ledger](../claim-evidence-ledger.md), [model registry](../model-registry.md), [example registry](../example-registry.md), [figure registry](../figure-registry.md), [source-salvage map](../source-salvage-map.md), and shared planning controls into an executable development program. It does not allocate new stable IDs or advance an evidence state by editorial declaration.

The governing sequence is:

> accepted chapter brief → normalized control records → focused research and evidence table → specialist decision → controlled passage salvage → source-grounded draft → chapter and book-level review

Research may proceed in parallel where dependencies permit. Promotion of claims, models, examples, figures, and passages remains record-specific. Reader-facing prose may begin only when the load-bearing records for that passage have passed their required gates.

## Authorial directions already in force

The following directions do not need to be reopened before execution:

- **Hard and soft are relational roles.** A hard role is relatively shape-preserving or structurally resolving. A soft role is relatively form-receiving or shape-conforming: it yields enough to receive surrounding form while retaining the relevant capacity for glide, excursion, containment, expansion, pressure response, recovery, or other task-specific deformation.
- **The traditional motivation should be visible early.** Book I should say when the distinction is introduced that it is motivated by traditional Chinese medicine's hard/soft and Yin/Yang concepts. The mechanical vocabulary is a flexible project translation, not a claim of definitional identity, complete historical equivalence, or one-to-one scientific validation. Detailed correspondence remains later-book work under the [later-book seam table](later-book-seam-table.md).
- **The Chapter 13 non-lumen comparison is flexor tendon–subsynovial connective tissue relative glide.** Median-nerve excursion remains a reserve rather than a competing primary example.
- **Chapter 24 tests maintained constraint in the thoracic-outlet region.** Arterial, venous, and neural distinctions may be used when the relevant quantities, chronology, evidence, or alternatives differ. The chapter does not need to teach a taxonomy of thoracic-outlet syndromes, and no subtype distinction is required merely for completeness.
- **Later hypotheses remain preserved.** Preferred configuration, signed generalized modes, posterior necessity, TCM correspondence, breath geometry, and practice are stated generously at their intended seams but do not carry Book I's foundational or medical argument.

These are authorial and editorial directions. They do not substitute for the remaining formal, anatomical, measurement, evidentiary, clinical, historical, provenance, rights, or safety reviews.

## Four development lanes

The work should be tracked in four distinct lanes. Completion in one lane never silently advances another.

| Lane | Purpose | Typical artifacts | What the lane cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Internal preparation** | Make the intended argument, dependencies, vocabulary, models, examples, and open questions explicit | accepted brief, control-record delta, research questions, search plan, review assignments | evidentiary support, clinical validity, publication wording |
| **Evidence advancement** | Determine what sources support, qualify, oppose, or fail to resolve each consequential proposition | retrieval log, source table, claim-by-claim synthesis, model check, comparison record, specialist decision | readable chapter prose, pedagogical success, whole-book continuity |
| **Reader-facing drafting** | Explain only the accepted and appropriately labeled material in a coherent narrative | chapter draft, figures, captions, notes, references, explicit hypothesis and boundary language | publication approval or immunity from later correction |
| **Publication review** | Test the assembled book as a medical, mechanical, causal, ethical, accessible, and editorial whole | technical and medical review records, citation audit, rights audit, continuity and seam review, copyedit and layout proofs | stronger evidence than the underlying records contain |

An internal hypothesis may be coherent and important while remaining unready for assertive reader-facing use. In that case, the appropriate result is to retain it as a hypothesis, narrow its job, move it to a later book, or quarantine a defective expression—not to erase its conceptual kernel.

## Per-unit advancement record

Each chapter should have one compact advancement record maintained beside its brief or evidence pack. This is a status view over existing records, not a new competing ID system. It should answer:

1. Has the chapter brief been accepted as the current development contract?
2. Which claims, models, examples, figures, and later-book seams are load-bearing?
3. Which existing control records apply, and which additions or revisions are proposed?
4. Which evidence questions and causal arrows require retrieval?
5. Which specialist reviews are required before synthesis, drafting, and publication?
6. Which source-archive locations are candidates for controlled salvage?
7. Which items have been retained, narrowed, replaced, deferred, or quarantined, and why?
8. Is any reader-facing passage authorized to be drafted?
9. What would stop or replace the current chapter mechanism, example, figure, or wording?
10. What is the chapter's next concrete deliverable?

The advancement record should link rather than duplicate the authoritative brief, registry entries, evidence tables, decision records, and salvage sheets.

## Source retrieval and evidence-table standard

### Retrieval order

For each load-bearing claim, model relation, causal arrow, example, and figure:

1. state the exact question and the proposition that a source could support or disfavor;
2. define system, population, task or exposure, comparison, quantity, time window, and intended publication job;
3. search primary experimental or observational evidence for exact findings;
4. retrieve standards, consensus documents, guidelines, and authoritative definitions for current terminology and clinical boundaries;
5. use systematic reviews and other syntheses for field state, heterogeneity, negative evidence, and unresolved links;
6. use formal, computational, cadaveric, bench, or analogue models only for the relation and boundary they actually study;
7. retrieve competing explanations, noncorresponding findings, nulls, and disfavoring observations deliberately; and
8. record the search date, sources searched, query logic, inclusion decision, and literature-refresh requirement.

Internal notes, traditional sources, embodied observations, legacy prose, and generated artifacts may motivate a question or preserve provenance. They do not acquire empirical or clinical authority by appearing beside external evidence.

### Required source-table fields

Each evidence pack should use proposition-level rows with at least:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Record link | Chapter, claim/model/example/figure, and exact causal arrow or teaching job |
| Source identity | Full citation, persistent locator, source type, publication date, and retrieval date |
| System and boundary | Population or specimen, anatomical region, task/exposure, comparator, scale, and time |
| Quantity or observation | What was observed, measured, calculated, modeled, inferred, hypothesized, embodied, or reconstructed |
| Exact support | The narrow proposition the source supports, qualifies, opposes, or leaves unresolved |
| Direction and uncertainty | Effect direction, magnitude where meaningful, variability, confidence or precision, and relevant null |
| Methods and transfer limits | Design, measurement validity, assumptions, confounding, selection, external validity, and analogy limits |
| Alternatives | Reverse, reciprocal, common-cause, measurement, tissue-specific, and whole-person alternatives at the affected node |
| Publication use | Candidate wording, allowed evidence state, citation location, and prohibited extrapolation |
| Review and disposition | Specialist needed, retain/narrow/replace/defer/quarantine decision, rationale, and refresh trigger |
| Provenance and rights | Source-of-case status, permission or image-rights status where relevant, and any composite transformation |

### Evidence-pack synthesis

After retrieval, each pack should contain:

1. the selected question and chapter job;
2. inclusion, exclusion, and search record;
3. proposition-level source table;
4. claim-by-claim synthesis rather than a literature collage;
5. measurements, models, assumptions, and validation limits;
6. positive, negative, mixed, noncorresponding, and missing evidence;
7. alternatives and observations that would disfavor the preferred account;
8. source-form and provenance decision for every worked example;
9. specialist review comments and resolutions;
10. retain, narrow, replace, defer, or quarantine decisions; and
11. exact changes required in the ledgers, registries, brief, figures, and salvage sheet.

A literature search is complete enough for drafting only when it supports a bounded publication decision. A large bibliography without proposition-level linkage does not close an evidence gate.

## Promotion gates

### Claims

A claim may enter assertive prose only when its normalized statement, scope, system boundary, chain position, population or task, evidence rows, alternatives, disfavoring observations, wording, and required review are explicit. M2 assertive prose requires DEF, EVID, LINK, and ALT. M3 clinical material additionally requires CLIN and condition-appropriate review. A hypothesis may enter earlier only when its status, motivation, missing link, and non-load-bearing job are visible.

### Models

A model advances through the lifecycle already defined in the [model registry](../model-registry.md): captured → specified → checked → evidence-linked → validated within scope → publication-approved. Promotion requires a declared purpose, represented object, variables, units where applicable, boundary conditions, assumptions, dependencies, outputs, competing representations, validation strategy, failure conditions, and specialist review. Formal vocabulary must not make an exploratory analogy appear derived.
For hard/soft, the publication model must preserve the authorial relation: shape-preserving or structurally resolving roles in relation to form-receiving or shape-conforming roles. It must say that this distinction is **motivated by** TCM hard/soft and Yin/Yang concepts, not that the mechanical terms are definitionally identical to those traditions.

### Examples and worked narratives

An example advances only when its unique teaching job, source form, provenance, system boundary, task, causal chain, strongest endpoint, weakest arrow, alternatives, negative or noncorresponding comparison, null, ethical and privacy status, specialist review, and replacement trigger are known. A vivid example cannot repair an unsupported general claim.

Thoracic-outlet distinctions are optional comparison tools. They should be promoted only when structure-specific variables prevent evidence transfer or when chronology materially clarifies the selected mechanism. They are not a completeness requirement or the chapter's thesis.

### Figures

A figure advances only after its editorial job and linked claim, model, or example are stable enough to specify what the reader may infer. It then requires panel and label specifications, visual encoding of evidence states and uncertainty, caption obligations, technical review, medical review where exposed, accessibility text, provenance and rights, and a stated failure to avoid. A polished image cannot advance an underlying claim or model.

### Passages

A legacy passage advances only when its conceptual kernel has a current home, its propositions match accepted controls, its sources are independently verified, its wording does not exceed the accepted evidence state, and its provenance is recorded. Generated prose is never a source. A passage that contains one useful idea and several defective claims should be decomposed rather than accepted or rejected as a whole.

## Controlled passage salvage

### Governing rule

Salvage follows an accepted chapter brief. It does not determine the chapter architecture. Draft from accepted records and a clean page; do not revise an old generated chapter until it appears compliant.

Archival indexing may begin before research closes, but no phrase, claim, equation, citation, case detail, or figure concept is promoted into reader-facing work until its governing control and evidence decisions are known.

### Salvage sequence

1. Read the accepted brief, shared controls, and current control records before opening source candidates.
2. Retrieve *reuse with editing* candidates first, then conceptual sources and provenance leads.
3. Read paired incompatible wording and quarantined expressions before drafting so the rewrite does not reproduce the same error.
4. Decompose each candidate into propositions, images, examples, narrative moves, terminology, and conceptual kernels.
5. Compare each element against current claims, models, examples, figures, evidence states, later-book seams, and chapter job.
6. Record a disposition for each element: retain, rewrite, split, narrow, replace, defer, move to a later book, quarantine expression, or remove as pipeline contamination.
7. Promote only the accepted kernel, with verified sources and current wording; never copy planning keys, prompt instructions, invented equations, placeholder citations, or generated confidence.
8. Draft the chapter from the brief, evidence pack, control records, and salvage sheet rather than from the legacy file.
9. Run a post-draft provenance check that maps every salvaged phrase, case detail, equation, quotation, and figure concept to its origin and current authority.

### Salvage-sheet fields

Each chapter salvage sheet should record:

- source path and stable locator;
- source type and provenance class;
- raw conceptual kernel in concise paraphrase;
- proposed chapter and subsection job;
- linked claim, model, example, figure, or later-book seam;
- proposition-level evidence needed and current status;
- disposition and rationale;
- wording or category error to avoid;
- rights, quotation, privacy, or attribution obligation;
- replacement or later-book destination where applicable; and
- post-draft verification status.

The [source-salvage map](../source-salvage-map.md) remains the book-wide inventory. Chapter sheets make its dispositions executable at passage level.

## Stop, narrowing, and replacement criteria

Stop promotion of a claim, model, example, figure, or passage when any load-bearing condition below applies:

- the construct or system boundary cannot be defined at the required scale;
- quantities, dimensions, coordinate descriptions, or causal order are inconsistent;
- a consequential arrow has no evidence appropriate to the represented system and cannot be labeled honestly as hypothesis;
- evidence from one tissue, condition, population, task, or timescale is being pooled into another without a transfer argument;
- direct observation, reconstruction, modeling, inference, and embodied report cannot be distinguished;
- the preferred explanation has no serious alternative, negative case, null, or disfavoring observation;
- the example lacks provenance, duplicates another chapter's job, or cannot support its strongest intended endpoint;
- a clinical or safety reviewer rejects the case form, wording, scope, or implied reader action;
- a figure would imply more certainty than its underlying records or cannot clear rights and accessibility review;
- the passage depends on generated prose, an unverifiable citation, placeholder reference, invented equation, or pipeline artifact;
- the material requires a later-book hypothesis to rescue Book I's argument; or
- the research burden is disproportionate to the chapter's unique editorial value.

A stop does not prescribe deletion. The permitted outcomes are:

1. retain the idea internally at its current epistemic level;
2. narrow the proposition, represented system, or publication job;
3. split a fused claim into independently researchable links;
4. replace the example or figure while preserving the conceptual question;
5. move the material to its later-book destination;
6. quarantine defective wording while preserving authorial provenance; or
7. remove it from reader-facing Book I when it has no necessary job.

Case-specific replacement decisions follow the [worked-narrative selection control](worked-narrative-selection-and-comparison.md). If objective arterial TOS cannot clear medical, provenance, source-linkage, or figure-rights review, replace it as the Chapter 24 lead rather than weakening the gates. Preserve the arterial, venous, and neural material in the research inventory at their actual evidence states.

Reader-facing drafting stops whenever an unresolved item is load-bearing to the paragraph, figure, chapter promise, or handoff. Non-load-bearing open questions may remain when they are labeled and do not make the surrounding conclusion appear stronger.

## Commit-sized milestones

Each commit should represent one coherent advancement that can be reviewed and reverted without untangling unrelated states. Push after each accepted commit so the repository remains the durable coordination record.

Good commit units include:

- one brief-acceptance or cross-book control-delta docket;
- one focused evidence pack together with the exact linked ledger and registry updates it justifies;
- one specialist-review resolution batch for a named artifact;
- one chapter salvage sheet or one tightly related source family;
- one model, example, or figure promotion decision with its evidence links;
- one reader-facing chapter draft with complete notes and a post-draft provenance check;
- one figure batch only when all figures share an accepted model and review path;
- one Part-level continuity, repetition, or causal-chain audit; or
- one publication-review resolution category.

Avoid commits that mix speculative research notes, evidence-state advancement, broad prose rewrites, unrelated figures, and layout changes. An evidence pack and its directly justified control-record updates belong together; unrelated chapter research does not. Before each commit, validate links, inspect the diff for planning contamination or accidental source edits, and state which gate or deliverable the commit closes.
