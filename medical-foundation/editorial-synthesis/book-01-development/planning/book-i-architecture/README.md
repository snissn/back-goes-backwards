# Book I strict architectural outline

**Status:** architectural rewrite in editorial stabilization

**Authority:** this package governs the next rewrite of Book I. The accepted series boundary remains authoritative for the division among Books I–V. The current first draft remains a preserved source archive, not the prose authority for this outline.

## Purpose

This package converts the accepted Book I structure into units small and definite enough to draft, review, move, or remove without allowing a chapter's general theme to substitute for an argument. It preserves the book's six-Part, thirty-chapter macro-architecture while refining the internal sequence.

The architectural outline and the prose-disposition crosswalk solve different problems:

- the Part files state what the next manuscript is meant to teach, in what order, and through which finite units;
- `prose-disposition-crosswalk.md` accounts for every reader-facing content block and the assembled bibliography in the current first draft and records whether each source object stays, moves, merges, is rewritten, is deferred, is rebuilt, or leaves the manuscript;
- `editorial-stabilization-record.md` records the review rounds that this architecture survives before redrafting begins.

No prose in the source draft is deleted by this work. A disposition is an editorial decision about the next manuscript, not a mutation of the archive.

## Constructive governing argument

> The human body is a living biomechanical system. It continuously organizes mechanical demand through changing relations among shape-preserving, form-receiving, active, tensile, sliding, and pressure-mediated structures. Configuration and history determine how external demand becomes local exposure; tissue state, capacity, recovery, and the whole person's circumstances determine what follows. Tracing that mechanical history can make medical reasoning more complete.

The book develops that argument in six affirmative movements:

1. **Mechanical foundations:** embodied life continuously generates and receives mechanical demand, and that demand can be described precisely.
2. **Architecture of support:** living support is a coordinated division of mechanical labor; posterior anatomy is one large and consequential participant in it.
3. **Load management:** viable support can change through six identifiable families: geometric redistribution; local concentration; task/segment instability; discoordination or mistimed handoff; maintained deformation/lost excursion; and compensatory rerouting.
4. **Medical meaning:** a mechanical history becomes medically informative by specifying the receiver, exposure, response, chronology, alternatives, and whole-person context.
5. **Worked narratives:** a useful framework clarifies real examples by making their chains more explicit, comparable, and testable.
6. **Reasoning and development:** the framework can improve questions, measurement, education, design, and research while the stronger preferred-configuration program is developed in later books.

## Reader-facing and editorial layers

Claim discipline remains strict, but it belongs primarily in the architecture, evidence ledger, notes, and review process. The reader-facing argument should normally lead with the positive proposition and explain its mechanism or usefulness. It should not repeatedly define the proposition through catalogs of what it does not mean.

Each unit therefore separates four layers:

1. **Proposition:** the affirmative idea the reader should retain.
2. **Development:** the minimum sequence of facts, distinctions, and reasoning needed to earn it.
3. **Evidence or example:** the concrete material that carries the explanation.
4. **Editorial boundary:** a concise internal guardrail. This may become one sentence, a note, a comparison, or no visible prose in the eventual draft.

Negative or contrastive prose is warranted when it performs one of four jobs: correcting a likely category error, distinguishing adjacent mechanisms, stating a real alternative, or marking the limit of evidence at a consequential bridge. It is not the default rhythm of exposition.

## Drafting-unit contract

Every numbered unit in the Part files is an independently draftable assignment. Most explanatory units should contain 300–650 reader-body words. Compact orientation or application bridges may contain 250–450 words; openings, conclusions, and short Part bridges may contain 180–450 words. No unit may exceed 650 words without an explicit architectural exception; otherwise a longer treatment must be split before drafting rather than allowed to accumulate several hidden jobs.

Each unit must provide:

- one reader-facing heading that states or names its subject directly;
- one affirmative proposition;
- two to five required content moves in their intended order;
- one declared evidence, example, figure, or comparison burden;
- one continuity statement identifying what the unit inherits and what it hands forward;
- one editorial boundary when the claim would otherwise expand beyond its job; and
- a target word range.

A drafting agent may improve sentences, transitions, and examples. It may not add a new mechanism, medical consequence, later-book theory, protocol, or literature claim merely because it fits the topic. Such material returns to the architecture or evidence ledger first.

## Unit states and source dispositions

The crosswalk uses the following controlled dispositions:

- **retain:** the content and location are sound; rewrite only for voice, compression, and continuity;
- **retain and split:** the substance remains, but the present block contains more than one drafting job;
- **move:** the material belongs in another named unit;
- **merge:** the material is useful but duplicates another treatment and should survive once;
- **rewrite constructively:** the underlying distinction remains, but defensive or exclusion-led exposition should be rebuilt around an affirmative proposition;
- **defer:** the material belongs in a later book or later development layer;
- **quarantine:** the idea remains in the project archive but has no present Book I destination pending definition, research, or author review;
- **retire duplicate:** no unique content remains after the named destination absorbs it.

The disposition column may join base decisions when one source block has several destinations. Treatment modifiers such as **split**, **compress**, **expand**, **rename**, **rebuild**, or **as hypothesis** describe how the retained material changes; they do not create an unnamed destination or a new epistemic state.

No unit is dispositioned merely because it is speculative. Speculation can remain when Book I needs to name the research program; its label, dependency, and evidentiary burden must be explicit.

## Book-level continuity device

One ordinary transfer remains the primary neutral task through Parts I and II: a person moves an object from one support to another and carries it several steps, with strategy, assistance, environment, and body state allowed to vary. Later Parts may recall it briefly when it performs a unique comparative or closing job; their principal examples should be the load-family, medical, and worked-case objects they introduce.

| Part | Use of the recurring task |
|---|---|
| I | Name system boundaries, quantities, roles, configuration, and candidate paths. |
| II | Follow the same demand through cooperating anatomical carriers and posterior participation. |
| III | Retire the full example after Chapter 8; use only a brief matched task where endpoint equivalence genuinely clarifies a route state. |
| IV | Use established medical examples and one hypothetical endpoint rather than reopening the object transfer. |
| V | Use the four case systems as the narrative center. |
| VI | Recall the original transfer in Chapter 30 to close the book and open the wider hypothesis. |

The object transfer should appear where it performs one of these jobs, not as a ritual callback at every chapter ending.

## Evidence and example discipline

Every explanatory chain uses the same inspectable sequence:

> task and environment → body state and configuration → available carriers and distribution → local mechanical exposure → tissue or system response over time → function, finding, symptom, or clinical interpretation

The chain is a scaffold, not a requirement that every chapter reach the final node. Units must name where their evidence begins and ends. Worked examples must also include chronology, competing explanations, and a result that would narrow or stop the proposed account.

Worked-case records and comparison tables keep five fields distinct:

1. **evidence provenance:** the exact I.8 state—observed, reported, measured, calculated, modeled, inferred, reconstructed, embodied, hypothesized, or unknown;
2. **scenario construction:** for example, a multistudy reconstruction, teaching composite, or stipulated hypothetical;
3. **empirical comparison result:** positive, null, mixed, or not applicable under a declared comparison;
4. **inferential disposition:** supported, narrowed, disfavored, or unresolved within the declared domain; and
5. **bounded output:** the mechanical relation, researchable interval, structured uncertainty, or stopping decision the case earns.

A case record contains all five fields. Scenario construction and bounded output describe the case as a whole; individual links carry provenance and, where relevant, an empirical comparison result and an inferential disposition. A comparison may be not applicable. None of these labels substitutes for another: a reconstructed relation is not automatically supported or disfavored, a null comparison is not an evidence-provenance state, and a stipulated scenario is not an observation.

Sources remain assigned by exact job:

- anatomy establishes structures and relations;
- mechanics establishes or models quantities and distributions;
- mechanobiology connects defined exposure to defined response;
- clinical evidence addresses findings, symptoms, diagnoses, prognosis, or outcomes;
- experience and traditional sources may define observations or organize hypotheses without borrowing another evidence type's status.

## Readiness gate for redrafting

Redrafting begins only after the package passes all of the following:

1. **Structural review:** every unit has one job; chapter and Part sequences have no missing premise or avoidable duplication.
2. **Developmental review:** every chapter changes the reader's understanding and earns the next chapter.
3. **Constructive-framing review:** the positive thesis carries the prose; boundaries remain visible without dominating the voice.
4. **Continuity review:** terminology, examples, proposition levels, and Book I/II boundaries remain stable across the whole volume.
5. **Disposition review:** every current prose block has one explicit destination or retirement decision, and no unique idea disappears silently.
6. **Production review:** unit IDs, target lengths, citations, figures, cross-references, and drafting inputs can be validated mechanically.

Stabilization does not mean that every sentence or source is final. It means the manuscript can be redrafted without asking each local drafting pass to rediscover the book's structure.
