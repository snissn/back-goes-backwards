# Book I architecture editorial-stabilization record

**Status:** active

This record tracks review rounds on the strict architecture. A round is structural only when it changes ownership, sequence, scope, or disposition. Wording and formatting corrections do not reset structural stabilization.

## Stabilization criteria

The architecture is ready for redrafting when:

- every subsection teaches one definite thing and begins from an affirmative proposition;
- every chapter has a unique job, reader movement, opening, synthesis, and forward handoff;
- every protected Book I idea has one visible owner and payoff;
- later chapters apply established definitions rather than teaching them again;
- every current reader-facing prose block has a destination or explicit retirement;
- every deferred idea has a named later-book or quarantine destination;
- no figure, example family, evidence cluster, or notes block is orphaned;
- the Book I/II seam remains non-load-bearing in both directions; and
- two consecutive independent developmental reviews require no structural revision.

- Claim boundaries should remain attached to every drafting unit as editorial controls but appear in reader prose only when they define the concept or materially change interpretation.
