# Reversible Quarantine

**Status:** initial editorial quarantine

Quarantine applies to a particular formulation, equation, protocol, citation, or pipeline artifact. It does not automatically apply to the conceptual kernel underneath it.

The first synthesis assigns every substantive source obligation to an active outline unit or internal inventory. No substantive concept is held only in quarantine. The records below prevent reader-facing contamination while preserving the originating idea, its provenance, and a route back into the project.

## Reason codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Q:claim-overreach` | Wording presents a conditional or unreviewed claim as settled or universal |
| `Q:protocol-before-review` | A test, cue, treatment, or recommendation is presented before evidence, safety, scope, and audience review |
| `Q:provenance-unclear` | The formulation's authorial, generated, historical, or evidentiary origin is not sufficiently clear |
| `Q:model-conflation` | Distinct models, variables, dimensions, or correspondence types are treated as identical without derivation |
| `Q:internal-conflict` | Source variants conflict or have not yet been reconciled |
| `Q:unsupported-equation` | Mathematical form is dimensionally inconsistent, undefined, or not derived from the stated model |
| `Q:pipeline-contamination` | Editorial instructions, prompts, placeholders, or renderer artifacts appear as book prose |
| `Q:audience-mismatch` | Useful internal, professional, or research material is in the wrong publication layer |

## Review rule

Quarantine records remain open until joint review accepts their protected kernel and re-entry condition. A later decision may normalize and reintegrate the wording, defer it to a more suitable product, or retire the formulation. Retirement is explicit and reversible; source material is never silently deleted.
