# *The Back Goes Backwards*, Book III: The Body's Coordinates

**Status:** Provisional synthesized outline

## Editorial role

Book III asks whether the axial, rotational, and lateral modes developed mechanically in Book II correspond meaningfully to Yin/Yang, the Six Divisions, paired channels, bandhas, and selected embodied traditions. It reconstructs traditional maps before translating them and preserves multiple mapping layers where the source program has not yet chosen a final reconciliation.

Traditional observation, authorial correspondence, anatomical analogy, formal mechanics, physiological mechanism, and medical implication remain distinguishable. That distinction permits the book to retain the complete hypothesis generously while making the work required of each layer explicit.

## Provisional thesis

Axial, rotational, and lateral organization can be treated as three signed mechanical dimensions of the body. Yin and Yang, the Six Divisions, paired channels, bandhas, and related traditional maps may preserve structured observations about those dimensions. This book reconstructs each map on its own terms and then develops explicit mechanical correspondences without requiring traditional and biomechanical vocabularies to collapse into one another.

The proposed correspondences are a research program. Their inclusion preserves the full model for study; it does not imply that historical provenance, anatomical mechanism, or clinical relevance has already been established.
