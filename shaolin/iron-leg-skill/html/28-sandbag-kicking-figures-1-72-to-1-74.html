Step 1
Side kick contact
Action
Place a controlled side kick into the hanging bag and retract without losing posture.
Key point
Let the bag hang freely and keep the support foot able to pivot.
1-72
Step 2
Front kick contact
Action
Drive the foot forward into the center of the bag, then return to balance.
Key point
Keep the toes and ankle organized for the chosen contact surface.
1-73
