Follow each row left to right.A linked turning jump. Learn the stepping arc and landing before adding the aerial strike.
Step 1
Arc-step and load
Action
From the upright stance, arc-step the right foot forward with the toe turned outward. Turn right and swing both arms behind.
Key point
Keep the stepping heel grounded and the route clear.

Step 2
Jump and turn
Action
Push from the right foot, sweep the left leg upward to the right, and clap the palms overhead while continuing the right turn.
Key point
Use the arms and lead leg to organize the rotation.

Step 3
Sweep and strike
Action
In the air, bend the left knee and sweep the right leg upward and outward. Strike the right sole with the hands in sequence.

Step 4
Land and absorb
Action
Lower the feet in sequence or together and settle into a stable upright landing.
Key point
Let the forefeet touch first, bend the knees to absorb impact, and regain balance.
