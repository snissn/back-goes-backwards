`吐纳` (*tu-na*) literally names exhalation and inhalation. It is retained in the main title because it denotes a coordinated breathing practice, not merely ordinary breathing.

### Breath-translation rules

Breath wording is preserved literally and marked in bold:

- `呼吸自然` / `自然吐纳` — **BREATHE NATURALLY**
- `以鼻深吸` — **INHALE DEEPLY THROUGH THE NOSE**
- `以鼻吸气` — **INHALE THROUGH THE NOSE**
- `吸气不停` / `深吸不停` — **CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION**
- `吸气至满` / `深吸至满` — **INHALE UNTIL FULL**
- `以鼻长呼` — **EXHALE LONG THROUGH THE NOSE**
- `以鼻呼气` — **EXHALE THROUGH THE NOSE**
- `呼气不停` — **CONTINUE EXHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION**
- `长呼至尽` — **EXHALE LONG UNTIL THE EXHALATION IS FINISHED**

No `屏息`, `闭气`, or other instruction to hold the breath appears in these pages. In particular, `吸气不停` means that the inhalation continues; it must not be silently changed into a breath hold. Where a later movement plainly belongs to an existing breath phase but the source supplies no new breath phrase, the translation says **no new breath cue in the source** rather than inventing one.

# I. `站法吐纳功` — Standing-Method Tu-Na Exercise

## `【练法】` — Method

1. **Source:** `两脚并步，正身直立；两掌垂于体侧；呼吸自然。目视前方。（图2-1）`

   **Translation — BREATHE NATURALLY:** Stand upright with both feet together and the torso straight. Let both palms hang at the sides of the body. Breathe naturally. Gaze forward. *(Figure 2-1.)*

2. **Source:** `左脚向左横开一步，两脚间距略比肩宽；同时，收两掌抱于丹田（小腹），右掌在外。定势后不动，深吸长呼数次，次数自定。（图2-2）`

   **Translation — REPEATED DEEP INHALES AND LONG EXHALES:** Step the left foot one step sideways to the left, leaving the feet slightly more than shoulder-width apart. At the same time, draw both palms in and hold them at the *dantian* (lower abdomen), with the right palm on the outside. Once the posture is set, remain still and perform several deep inhalations and long exhalations; choose the number yourself. *(Figure 2-2.)*

3. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌分开向上提起，掌心向下，约与耳平。随即，以鼻长呼；两臂向两侧缓缓摊开，两掌置于两胯外侧，掌心向外，虎口向后。（图2-3）`

   **Translation — INHALE, THEN EXHALE:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Separate the palms and raise them upward, palms facing down, to approximately ear level. Immediately exhale long through the nose. Slowly spread both arms out to the sides, placing the palms outside the hips, palms facing outward and the tiger's mouths of the hands pointing backward. *(Figure 2-3.)*

4. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌向外上提展臂，掌心向上，指尖向外，高与肩平。（图2-4）`

   **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Raise the palms outward and upward while extending the arms, palms facing up and fingertips pointing outward, at shoulder height. *(Figure 2-4.)*

5. **Source:** `两掌屈肘里合，至额前，指尖相对，掌心向下；吸气至满。（图2-5）`

   **Translation — CONTINUE THE SAME INHALATION UNTIL FULL:** Bend the elbows and bring the palms together inward to the front of the forehead, fingertips facing one another and palms facing down. Inhale until full. *(Figure 2-5.)*

6. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两掌沿面前下按，经胸前至小腹。（图2-6）`

   **Translation — EXHALE:** Exhale long through the nose. Press both palms downward in front of the face, passing the front of the chest and continuing to the lower abdomen. *(Figure 2-6.)*

7. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌外旋，掌心向上，提至两腋前，指尖向前。目视前方。（图2-7）`

   **Translation — INHALE:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Externally rotate both palms so that they face up, and raise them to the front of the armpits, fingertips pointing forward. Gaze forward. *(Figure 2-7.)*

8. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；转掌，向前伸臂推出，腕高与肩平，掌心向前，指尖向上。（图2-8）`

   **Translation — EXHALE:** Exhale long through the nose. Turn the palms, extend the arms forward, and push out. The wrists are at shoulder height, palms facing forward and fingertips pointing up. *(Figure 2-8 and its supplementary side view.)*

9. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌向左右展臂分开，高约平肩，虎口向下，掌心向后。（图2-9）`

   **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Spread both arms apart to the left and right at approximately shoulder height, with the tiger's mouths pointing down and the palms facing backward. *(Figure 2-9.)*

10. **Source:** `吸气不停；两掌屈指握拳，向下收抱腰间，拳心向上。目视前方。（图2-10）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Curl the fingers into fists and draw them downward to the waist, with the insides of the fists facing up. Gaze forward. *(Figure 2-10.)*

11. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两拳变掌，自体侧弧形上举至额前，掌心向下，五指分开，指尖相对。（图2-11）`

    **Translation — EXHALE BEGINS:** Exhale long through the nose. Open both fists into palms and raise them in an arc from the sides of the body to the front of the forehead, palms facing down, all five fingers spread, and fingertips facing one another. *(Figure 2-11.)*

12. **Source:** `呼气不停；两掌下按过胸，直至小腹前。（图2-12）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE EXHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Press both palms down past the chest until they reach the front of the lower abdomen. *(Figure 2-12.)*

13. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌向左右分开，展臂平肩，掌心向上，指尖向外。（图2-13）`

    **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Separate the palms to the left and right, extending the arms at shoulder height, palms facing up and fingertips pointing outward. *(Figure 2-13.)*

14. **Source:** `吸气不停；两掌自两侧继续上举划弧，至头顶上方，两掌心相贴合十，指尖向上。目视前方。（图2-14）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Continue raising both palms from the sides in an arc until they are above the head; place the palms together in a prayer gesture, fingertips pointing upward. Gaze forward. *(Figure 2-14.)*

15. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；合掌屈肘沉臂，肘与肩平，两中指端约与鼻尖平，两臂成半弧状。（图2-15）`

    **Translation — EXHALE:** Exhale long through the nose. Keeping the palms together, bend the elbows and sink the arms. The elbows are level with the shoulders, the tips of the middle fingers are approximately level with the tip of the nose, and the arms form a half-arc. *(Figure 2-15 and its supplementary side view.)*

16. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；合掌缓缓内转，指尖向里对准鼻尖，虎口向下。（图2-16）`

    **Translation — INHALE:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Slowly rotate the joined palms inward so that the fingertips point inward and align with the tip of the nose, with the tiger's mouths pointing down. *(Figure 2-16.)*

17. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两掌根分开，掌心向前，拇指端相对，食指端相接，伸臂向前推出至臂直。（图2-17）`

    **Translation — EXHALE BEGINS:** Exhale long through the nose. Separate the heels of the palms, palms facing forward, thumb tips facing one another, and index-finger tips touching. Extend the arms and push forward until the arms are straight. *(Figure 2-17 and its supplementary side view.)*

18. **Source:** `呼气不停；两掌向左右分开，两腕平肩，坐腕竖掌，指尖向上，掌心向外。（图2-18）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE EXHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Separate the palms to the left and right, wrists level with the shoulders. Set the wrists and hold the palms upright, fingertips pointing up and palms facing outward. *(Figure 2-18.)*

19. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌下沉，平伸掌指，使肩、臂、掌成一直线。（图2-19）`

    **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Sink both palms and extend the palms and fingers horizontally so that the shoulders, arms, and palms form one straight line. *(Figure 2-19.)*

20. **Source:** `吸气不停；两掌翻掌，掌心向上，指尖向外。目视前方。（图2-20）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Turn both palms so that they face up, fingertips pointing outward. Gaze forward. *(Figure 2-20.)*

21. **Source:** `吸气不停；两掌上举里合，至额前上方，指尖相对，掌心向下。（图2-21）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Raise both palms and draw them inward to above the forehead, fingertips facing one another and palms facing down. *(Figure 2-21.)*

22. **Source:** `吸气不停；两掌下按，经胸前压至腰侧，指尖相对。（图2-22）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Press both palms downward, passing the front of the chest and pressing to the sides of the waist, with the fingertips facing one another. *(Figure 2-22.)*

23. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两掌变拳，向前抬起，直臂与肩平，拳心向上。（图2-23）`

    **Translation — EXHALE:** Exhale long through the nose. Form both palms into fists and raise them forward, arms straight and level with the shoulders, with the insides of the fists facing up. *(Figure 2-23 and its supplementary side view.)*

24. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两拳内翻后收，拳心向下，收至两腰侧。（图2-24）`

    **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Turn both fists inward and draw them back, the insides of the fists facing down, until they reach the two sides of the waist. *(Figure 2-24.)*

25. **Source:** `吸气不停；两拳上提胸前，拳面相对，拳心向下。（图2-25）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Raise both fists to the front of the chest, the faces of the fists toward one another and the insides of the fists facing down. *(Figure 2-25.)*

26. **Source:** `吸气不停；两拳向两肩前拉开；两脚跟同时提悬，两前脚掌撑地。（图2-26）`

    **Translation — CONTINUE INHALING WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Pull the fists apart to the front of the shoulders. At the same time, lift and suspend both heels, supporting the body on the balls of the feet. *(Figure 2-26 and its supplementary side view.)*

27. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两拳缓缓收回腰间，拳心向下；放下脚跟。（图2-27）`

    **Translation — EXHALE:** Exhale long through the nose. Slowly return both fists to the waist, the insides of the fists facing down, and lower the heels. *(Figure 2-27.)*

28. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两拳向前平肩推出，上身微后倾，拳心向上。（图2-28）`

    **Translation — INHALE:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Push both fists forward at shoulder height, inclining the upper body slightly backward, with the insides of the fists facing up. *(Figure 2-28.)*

29. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两拳变掌，垂放体侧。最后，放松全身，调匀呼吸，收势。（图2-29）`

    **Translation — EXHALE, THEN EVEN THE BREATH:** Exhale long through the nose. Open both fists into palms and lower them to hang at the sides of the body. Finally, relax the whole body, regulate the breath until it is even, and close the exercise. *(Figure 2-29.)*

# II. `坐法吐纳功` — Seated-Method Tu-Na Exercises

## 1. `四聪运转` — Circulating the Four Cong Points

The title literally reads “Circulation of the Four Intelligences.” The instructions identify the four points as `四神聪`, *Sishencong* (EX-HN1).

1. **Source:** `单盘坐定；两掌抚于两膝盖上，上体端正。（图2-30）`

   **Translation — no explicit breath cue:** Settle into a half-lotus/single-cross-legged seated posture. Rest both palms on the knees and keep the upper body upright. *(Figure 2-30.)*

2. **Source:** `用手掌劳宫穴，对准头顶百会穴，则全掌覆于四神聪之上（男左掌在下，女右掌在下），两掌重叠；配合吐纳，左转数次，右转数次，每转一圈，深长呼吸一次（如体力允许，可多转）。（图2-31）`

   **Translation — ONE DEEP, LONG BREATH PER COMPLETE CIRCLE:** Align the Laogong point (PC8) in the palm with the Baihui point (GV20) at the crown of the head, so that the whole palm covers the four Sishencong points. For men, place the left palm underneath; for women, place the right palm underneath. Overlap the two palms. Coordinate the rotations with the breath: rotate to the left several times and to the right several times, taking one deep, long breath for every complete circle. If physical strength permits, perform more rotations. *(Figure 2-31.)*

**Source:** `始而头部有凉热感或麻胀感，继而有轻松感。`

**Translation:** At first, the head may feel cool or warm, or may feel numb and distended; afterward, it may feel light and relaxed.

**Source:** `运转数次后，再用手掌在头顶重按数次，以动作缓慢、呼吸深长为要。`

**Translation — SLOW MOVEMENT; DEEP, LONG BREATHING:** After several rotations, use the palms to press firmly on the crown of the head several times. Slow movement and deep, long breathing are essential.

**Source:** `注：这一节多为穴位按摩，穴位图在后，习者自行参照掌握。每一势中就不再详述穴位。`

**Translation — Note:** This section consists mostly of acupoint massage. The acupoint chart appears later; practitioners should consult it and learn the locations themselves. Therefore, the acupoints will not be described in detail in every posture.

**Breath map:** The source does not assign inhale and exhale to separate halves of a circle. It says only that every complete circle receives **one deep, long breath**. Do not invent a left/right inhale–exhale division.

## 2. `日月通明` — Sun and Moon Shining Bright

1. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌轻抚面部，掌心向里，掌根放在颧骨下，掌心放在颧骨上。（图2-32）`

   **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Lightly place both palms against the face, palms facing inward, with the heels of the palms below the cheekbones and the centers of the palms over the cheekbones. *(Figure 2-32.)*

2. **Source:** `掌向上推，使中指达到前顶穴处，如洗脸之势；吸气至满。（图2-33）`

   **Translation — CONTINUE THE SAME INHALATION UNTIL FULL:** Push the palms upward until the middle fingers reach the Qianding point (GV21), as in the motion of washing the face. Inhale until full. *(Figure 2-33.)*

3. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两掌按擦面部下行。（图2-34）`

   **Translation — EXHALE BEGINS:** Exhale long through the nose. Press and rub both palms downward over the face. *(Figure 2-34.)*

4. **Source:** `两掌至胸前之乳中穴，逐渐转为指尖向下；行至腹股沟之气冲穴变横掌摩腹，气感可达足次趾端之足阳明胃经的厉兑穴。（图2-35）`

   **Translation — no new breath cue in the source; this movement follows step 3's exhale:** When the palms reach the Ruzhong points (ST17) on the chest, gradually turn them so that the fingertips point downward. On reaching the Qichong points (ST30) in the groin, turn the palms horizontal and rub the abdomen. The source says that the sensation of *qi* may reach Lidui (ST45), at the tip of the second toe, along the Foot-Yangming Stomach Channel. *(Figure 2-35.)*

**Source:** `一呼一吸为一次，反复练习。`

**Translation:** One exhalation and one inhalation constitute one repetition; practice repeatedly. The source states the pair in that order: **exhalation, then inhalation**.

**Source:** `这一动作涉及许多经络，而收效较大者是足阳明胃经。`

**Translation:** This movement involves many channels; the source says that the Foot-Yangming Stomach Channel receives the greatest effect.

**Breath map:** Steps 1–2 are one inhale, completed at fullness. Steps 3–4 form the downward phase; the exhale is explicitly started in step 3, while step 4 supplies no new breath wording.

## 3. `双阳下降` — Descent of the Two Yang Channels

1. **Source:** `以鼻深吸；两掌由身侧上举，以小指腹按在两眼角内侧的睛明穴上。（图2-36）`

   **Translation — INHALE BEGINS:** Inhale deeply through the nose. Raise both palms from the sides of the body and press the pads of the little fingers onto the Jingming points (BL1) at the inner corners of the eyes. *(Figure 2-36.)*

2. **Source:** `深吸不停；两掌上行顶巅。（图2-37）`

   **Translation — CONTINUE THE DEEP INHALATION WITHOUT INTERRUPTION:** Move both palms upward over the crown of the head. *(Figure 2-37.)*

3. **Source:** `两手向下、向后循按至胆经之风池穴；吸气至满。（图2-38）`

   **Translation — CONTINUE THE SAME INHALATION UNTIL FULL:** Trace and press both hands downward and backward to Fengchi (GB20) on the Gallbladder Channel. Inhale until full. *(Figure 2-38.)*

4. **Source:** `以鼻长呼；两掌翻转使掌心向里，经锁骨下行。（图2-39）`

   **Translation — EXHALE BEGINS:** Exhale long through the nose. Turn both palms so that they face inward and move them down past the collarbones. *(Figure 2-39.)*

5. **Source:** `两掌经过渊腋、日月、京门、环跳诸穴，直达足窍阴穴。（图2-40）`

## Fidelity notes

- Sensations, channel effects, and outcomes above are translated as claims made by the source text; they are not independently asserted medical advice.
- Gender-specific palm order in Exercise 1 is preserved exactly as printed: left palm underneath for men, right palm underneath for women.
- The unusually long standing inhalation across steps 19–22 is not a transcription error: steps 20, 21, and 22 each explicitly repeat `吸气不停`.
- The standing inhalation across steps 24–26 is likewise explicit.
- `一呼一吸` in Seated Exercise 2 is rendered in its printed order, “one exhalation and one inhalation.” Elsewhere, `一吸一呼` is rendered “one inhalation and one exhalation.”
- No breath timing has been added to Seated Exercises 6 or 10. Exercise 10 says only `自然吐纳`—breathe naturally.
