Figures 1–2
Count
one to five—or one to ten
Settle the mind and attend to the lower dantian. Gradually make the breath slower, longer, deeper, even, and comfortable. Silently count each breath; do not chase a high number.
1
Build slowly
about 15 minutes at first
Begin with roughly a quarter-hour. Increase gradually to 30 or 45 minutes; the source advises stopping at about one hour. Never force the posture or the breath.
