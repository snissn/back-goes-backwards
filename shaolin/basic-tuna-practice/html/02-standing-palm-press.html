Set up
breathe naturally
Stand with the feet about shoulder-width. Shift the upper body slightly forward, relax completely, let the hands hang at the sides, and lower the gaze.

Pattern A · Exhale
long · through the nose
Raise both hands from the sides to overhead. Keep the arms in a half-arc, palms down, and open the eyes.
