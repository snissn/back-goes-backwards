# Standing sequence — dense diagram grid

Compact US Letter edition of all 29 standing-sequence instructions across five pages
(six cards per page). Each bordered card places one individually excised original book
diagram, sized as large as the layout allows, above its exact English translation.

- `print/standing-sequence-dense-grid-letter.pdf` — complete print set
- `previews/contact-sheet.jpg` — all pages at a glance
- `figures/` — locally cleaned diagram crops

Layout: each card is exactly as tall as its figure plus its own caption, and the
remaining vertical slack is distributed evenly between rows, so no card carries dead
space beneath its text. The figure box is 2 in tall (up from the earlier 1.66 in).

Artwork cleanup is deliberately conservative and fully local — no figure has been
AI-redrawn. A gentle level stretch with a conservative threshold keeps light-gray
scan grime from binarizing into solid black blobs (which the earlier aggressive
threshold produced); despeckle drops stray specks; a couple of figures have a solid
corner scan-blob painted out with a white erase-box; and every crop is auto-trimmed to
its real content and re-centered, which also excises stray page numbers, bled captions,
and neighbouring figures.

`吸气不停` and `呼气不停` are rendered as continuation of the same inhalation or exhalation **without interruption**; neither phrase indicates holding the breath.
