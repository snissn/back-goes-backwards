# Image-generation prompt record

The base plates were produced with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. This record preserves the final prompt specification used to make the visual assets reproducible and auditable.

## Shared prompt specification

Every plate used this shared direction, followed by the asset-specific sequence below:

> Use case: scientific-educational exercise/acupressure infographic plate. Reconstruct the supplied Chinese manual figures with very high pose and route fidelity. Keep one consistent neutral adult practitioner in loose white traditional Shaolin training clothes. Use precise vintage Chinese martial-arts/medical-manual line art refined into clean modern instructional art: crisp charcoal contours, restrained warm-gray shading, warm ivory paper, and sparse muted-cinnabar movement arrows or point dots only where requested. Keep every head, hand, and foot visible. Leave generous blank label space. Absolutely no words, letters, Chinese characters, numerals, captions, logos, watermark, decorative title, invented equipment, or extra people.

## Asset-specific final prompts

### Standing 1 · Figures 2-1—2-6

Six panels, 2 × 3. Recreate the six referenced poses in order. Preserve stance width, palm height/direction, elbow bend, and posture. Figure 2-3 may use a faint ghosted arm path because the numbered step contains both raising and spreading the hands.

### Standing 2 · Figures 2-7—2-12

Six panels, 2 × 3: palms up beside armpits; forward vertical-palm push; arms spread shoulder-high with palms backward; fists to waist; open palms arc before forehead; palms press to lower abdomen.

### Standing 3 · Figures 2-13—2-18

Six panels, 2 × 3: arms shoulder-level palms up; arc overhead into prayer; joined palms sink to nose height; joined palms rotate inward toward nose; palm heels separate and arms push straight forward; hands separate sideways into vertical outward palms.

### Standing 4 · Figures 2-19—2-23

Five panels: arms and flat palms form a horizontal line; palms turn upward; hands rise inward above forehead, palms down; palms press to waist, fingertips facing; fists rise forward on straight shoulder-high arms, fist interiors up.

### Standing 5 · Figures 2-24—2-29

Six panels, 2 × 3: fists withdraw to waist, interiors down; lift before chest; pull before shoulders as both heels rise; fists return as heels lower; shoulder-high forward fist push with slight backward torso lean; open hands lower to a relaxed close.

### Seated 1 · Circulating the Four Cong Points

Four panels: half-lotus setup; overlapping palms flat on crown with a crown-location inset; rotate left; rotate right and press firmly. Preserve whole-palm coverage of the four crown points. One full circle remains undivided visually.

### Seated 2 · Sun and Moon Shining Bright

Four panels matching Figures 2-32—2-35: palms/heels over cheekbones; push upward like washing the face; rub downward over face; turn fingertips down at chest, descend to groin, then rub abdomen horizontally.

### Seated 3 · Descent of the Two Yang Channels

Five panels: little-finger pads at inner eye corners; palms over crown; trace to base-of-skull Fengchi; palms turn inward and descend past collarbones; continue bilaterally down ribs, hips, outer legs, and to toe tips. Show crown lifted, neck upright, waist relaxed, and hips sunk.

### Seated 4 · Turning at Tinggong

Three panels with closeups: sword fingers press in front of ears; push upward past ear/temple points; move downward while point-pressing or rubbing beside ears. Show two extended sword fingers correctly.

### Seated 5 · Circulation of the Five Wheels

Three panels with face insets: press below eye sockets; circle outward and upward across eye/brow/forehead route; move inward/down across brow to inner eye corners, then descend nose bridge.

### Seated 6 · Tap the Teeth Audibly

One seated figure plus three dental insets: relaxed separation, gentle vertical tooth-to-tooth contact, and relaxed close. Make clear that the action is tapping rather than grinding or clenching. No written sound effects.

### Seated 7 · Fire Refines the Golden Elixir

Five panels: foot edges meet and soles face up; toes rise and legs begin extending; hands travel from sole centers up inner-leg Kidney routes toward lower back; begin the Bladder-route return; continue to lateral fifth-toe tips.

### Seated 8 · Heat Dispersal at Heding

Final clean prompt: two large panels plus a knee/hand-placement inset. Palm center rests above the kneecap; index and ring fingers sit below it. No arrows, motion lines, route lines, colored dots, point markers, or colored marks anywhere. The opposite directions are added later as exact typeset captions.

### Seated 9 · Revolving Qian and Kun

Seven panels matching Figures 2-55—2-61: palms rise; rotate into shoulder-point claws; rub backward/down from armpits; palms at lower back then bow with arms back; arc palms forward/up; elbows bend with palms inward/fingertips down; spread fingers and push outward in a half-arc.

### Seated 10 · Azure Dragon Probes Its Head

Six panels: palms behind waist; trace down Bladder route; bend and follow outer-leg route to feet with thumbs at Gongsun; reach forward, grip toes, and rub sole centers; extend legs while withdrawing hands along inner legs; return hands behind waist.

### Seated 11 · Immortal Crane Soars

Five panels on a backless stool: relaxed arms; arms begin rising with sunk shoulders/dropped elbows and fingertips down; arms reach shoulder height with flat palms; arms begin falling; arms return slowly to sides. The motion is light and crane-like, without depicting a bird.
